2. Reduced reliability in DC devices: Ensuring the high availability and durability of DC components is essential for the widespread adoption of DC technologies.
3. Lack of use-cases in which DC is advantageous: Identifying applications where DC systems offer clear benefits over AC, such as reduced losses and simplified infrastructure, can help drive their adoption.
