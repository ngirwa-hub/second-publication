3. Lack of use-cases in which DC is advantageous: There are limited well-established use-cases demonstrating the advantages of DC technologies over traditional AC systems, making it challenging for potential adopters to justify the investment in DC infrastructure.

5. Lack of pilot projects: The absence of successful pilot projects using medium- and low-voltage DC technologies can make it difficult for potential adopters to assess the viability and benefits of such systems, making them hesitant to invest in DC infrastructure.
