--- phi4-subject-matter ---
3 - Feasible

This rating reflects that, based on the provided context and expert elicitation protocol within the Shift2DC initiative, medium- and low-voltage DC solutions are assessed to be generally feasible across data centers, buildings, industry, and ports. This conclusion is drawn from the planned demonstrations, feasibility studies, and integration of technologies such as smart cables, connectors, converters, and protection systems, which collectively support the adoption of DC power in these sectors. The involvement of experts and alignment with current standards further bolster this assessment. However, challenges such as interoperability, regulatory barriers, and initial infrastructure costs are acknowledged, hence the rating falls short of "very feasible."
