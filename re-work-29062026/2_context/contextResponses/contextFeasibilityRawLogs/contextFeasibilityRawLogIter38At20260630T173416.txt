--- phi4-normative ---
3 - Feasible

This rating reflects an assessment that, while there are challenges and uncertainties associated with the implementation of DC solutions across various sectors as outlined in the Shift2DC project, the overall feasibility is positive. The development of technologies like smart cables, connectors, converters, and system design tools indicates a strong foundation for medium- and low-voltage DC adoption. Furthermore, the targeted demonstrations in data centers, buildings, industry, and ports suggest a practical approach to testing and refining these solutions within real-world contexts.

The feasibility is bolstered by the project's comprehensive scope, which includes not only technological development but also regulatory alignment (e.g., compliance with Current/OS and ODCA standards), economic analysis tools, and stakeholder engagement strategies. The inclusion of smart technologies such as IoT-enabled circuit breakers and advanced simulation tools underscores an innovative approach to overcoming traditional barriers in DC systems.

However, the project is still in its early stages, which introduces uncertainties regarding user acceptance, integration challenges with existing infrastructure, and potential regulatory hurdles. These factors may temper the feasibility assessment but do not detract significantly from the overall positive outlook provided by the structured and multi-faceted approach of the Shift2DC initiative.

In summary, while acknowledging the complexity and nascent stage of widespread DC adoption, the project's strategic planning, technological advancements, and targeted demonstrations suggest a feasible path forward for integrating medium- and low-voltage DC solutions in the specified sectors. This holistic approach to addressing both technical and societal aspects underpins the assessment that these DC solutions are feasible for their intended applications.
