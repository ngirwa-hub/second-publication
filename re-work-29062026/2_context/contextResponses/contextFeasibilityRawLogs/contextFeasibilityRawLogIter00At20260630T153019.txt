--- phi4-generalist ---
3 - Feasible

This rating reflects an overall assessment that DC solutions, as outlined in the Shift2DC project, are feasible for implementation across the target sectors of data centers, buildings, industry, and ports. The project addresses key technological enablers such as smart cables, connectors, protection systems, converters, measurement devices, and design tools, which collectively support a transition to DC power. Furthermore, the planned demonstrations in diverse application areas provide practical insights into integration challenges and benefits, enhancing feasibility. However, while promising, there are still hurdles related to adoption rates, cost considerations, and technological maturity that prevent a "Very feasible" rating.

--- phi4-normative ---
3 - Feasible

This rating reflects an assessment that, while there are challenges and uncertainties associated with the adoption of DC solutions in the target sectors (data centers, buildings, industry, and ports), these solutions possess a reasonable degree of feasibility. The project’s focus on developing tools, simulations, prototypes, and demonstrator sites across various applications indicates progress towards practical implementation. However, given that the project is still in its early stages with limited direct user engagement, full-scale feasibility is not yet confirmed as "very feasible," hence a rating of 3 is appropriate to reflect moderate confidence in their eventual success within these sectors.
