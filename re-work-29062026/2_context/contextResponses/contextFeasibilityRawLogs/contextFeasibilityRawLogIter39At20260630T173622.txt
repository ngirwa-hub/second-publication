This rating reflects an assessment that, while there are challenges and uncertainties associated with the adoption of DC solutions in target sectors like data centers, buildings, industry, and ports, the Shift2DC project's comprehensive approach—encompassing technological development, demonstrations, feasibility studies, and expert input—supports a feasible path forward. --- phi4-subject-matter ---
3 - Feasible

This rating considers the comprehensive approach of the Shift2DC project in addressing technical, practical, and infrastructural challenges associated with implementing DC solutions across various sectors like data centers, buildings, industry, and ports. The integration of technologies such as smart cables, DC connectors, circuit breakers, converters, and measurement devices, alongside the development of simulation and design tools, suggests a strong foundation for feasibility. While full-scale adoption faces hurdles such as regulatory barriers and existing AC infrastructure compatibility, the project's strategic focus on demonstrators, expert input, and interoperability frameworks enhances its practical viability within these sectors.
