--- phi4-normative ---
3 - Feasible

This rating considers the comprehensive approach of the Shift2DC project, which includes a combination of technological innovations, feasibility studies, and demonstrator projects across various sectors. The initiative's focus on developing tools, prototypes with high technology readiness levels (TRLs), and practical demonstrations in data centers, buildings, industry, and ports indicates a well-rounded strategy to address both technical and societal aspects of DC adoption. While challenges remain, such as integration with existing infrastructure and market acceptance, the project's structured approach suggests that widespread adoption is feasible, albeit requiring concerted efforts across multiple fronts.

--- phi4-subject-matter ---
3 - Feasible

This rating reflects an assessment that, while there are challenges and uncertainties associated with medium- and low-voltage DC solutions in the target sectors of data centers, buildings, industry, and ports, the proposed technologies and demonstrations within the Shift2DC project suggest a reasonable likelihood of successful adoption. The development of tools, simulation platforms, and prototypes indicates progress toward overcoming technical barriers. However, given that many aspects are still under research or early demonstration phases, complete feasibility without addressing regulatory, interoperability, and infrastructural issues cannot be guaranteed at this time. Therefore, the overall assessment is "feasible" but not yet at a stage where it can be considered "very feasible."
