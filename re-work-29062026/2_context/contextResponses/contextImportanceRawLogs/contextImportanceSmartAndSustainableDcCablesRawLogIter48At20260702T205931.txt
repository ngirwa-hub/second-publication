The smart and sustainable DC cables are pivotal in the Shift2DC project because they address multiple critical aspects necessary for the broader acceptance and integration of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. Firstly, their design with durable and environmentally friendly materials ensures longevity and reduced environmental impact, which aligns with global sustainability goals and regulatory frameworks favoring greener technologies.

Secondly, these cables prioritize ergonomic use by mimicking standard AC cable handling, simplifying adoption across various demonstration sites such as data centers, buildings, industry setups, and ports. This user-friendly design reduces the learning curve and technical barriers for current infrastructure operators who are accustomed to AC systems, thus accelerating deployment and acceptance in existing networks.

Furthermore, their implementation is crucial in ensuring reliability and safety within DC grids, which is vital for gaining trust among stakeholders including utilities, manufacturers, and end-users. By addressing these key areas, smart and sustainable DC cables significantly contribute to overcoming the technical challenges associated with DC technology adoption, making them a cornerstone solution in advancing the Shift2DC initiative's objectives.
