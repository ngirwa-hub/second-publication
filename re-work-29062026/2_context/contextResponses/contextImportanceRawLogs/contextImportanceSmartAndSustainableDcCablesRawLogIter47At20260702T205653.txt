---

The "smart and sustainable DC cables" play a foundational role in the Shift2DC initiative, underpinning the adoption and efficiency of medium- and low-voltage DC solutions. Their design emphasizes durability and sustainability, aligning with global trends toward greener technologies. By mirroring the handling characteristics of standard AC cables, they significantly lower the barriers to widespread adoption across different sectors such as data centers, buildings, industries, and ports. This adaptability ensures that the transition to DC infrastructure can occur smoothly without necessitating extensive retraining or alterations in existing practices.

Furthermore, these cables facilitate the integration of renewable energy sources by providing a robust medium for power transmission, which is essential for projects like solar-powered microgrids in data centers and V2G systems. Their ability to support ergonomic use while maintaining performance standards makes them indispensable for modernizing electrical grids and enhancing their capacity to handle DC loads efficiently.

In addition to technical benefits, the sustainable aspect of these cables addresses environmental concerns, contributing to reduced carbon footprints and aligning with regulatory requirements for eco-friendly materials in construction and infrastructure projects. This dual focus on sustainability and usability positions smart and sustainable DC cables as a cornerstone technology within the Shift2DC project's broader goal of enabling widespread adoption of DC solutions.
