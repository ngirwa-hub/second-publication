---

The smart and sustainable DC cables play a pivotal role in advancing medium- and low-voltage DC technologies due to their ability to simplify adoption with ergonomic designs that mimic AC cable handling. Their importance is underscored by the need for durable materials that are environmentally friendly, which aligns with global sustainability goals. Furthermore, these cables support seamless integration across various demonstration sites (e.g., data centers, buildings, industry, and ports) within the Shift2DC project, ensuring consistent performance and reliability.

In practical terms, their use in real-world applications like microgrids or renewable energy systems highlights their ability to handle modern demands for efficiency and sustainability. The ergonomic design reduces training requirements and operational complexities, making them a vital component for widespread DC adoption. Therefore, their contribution to both the technical feasibility and environmental impact of DC projects makes them extremely important within this context.

Given these considerations, smart and sustainable DC cables are rated as "Very Important" (4) in facilitating the shift towards efficient, reliable, and environmentally friendly DC systems.
