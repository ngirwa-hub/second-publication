The emphasis on drag-and-drop usability significantly lowers the entry threshold for stakeholders who may not have deep technical expertise in DC systems, thereby accelerating adoption rates.

Moreover, the tool's capability to perform electrical sizing and techno-economic analysis is essential for evaluating the feasibility and cost-effectiveness of proposed DC solutions. This functionality supports decision-making processes by providing detailed insights into potential economic benefits and technical performance, which are critical factors in justifying investments in new technologies. By facilitating these analyses, the tool helps stakeholders make informed choices that align with both short-term operational goals and long-term sustainability objectives.

In addition to its practical applications, the DC solution design tool plays a strategic role in addressing mixed views and uncertainties highlighted in existing literature regarding medium- and low-voltage DC adoption. By standardizing design processes and offering robust simulation capabilities, it helps reconcile differing perspectives among experts by providing a common platform for evaluating various scenarios and outcomes. This convergence of expert opinions can lead to more cohesive strategies and policies that support the widespread implementation of DC technologies.

Overall, the DC solution design tool is instrumental in bridging the gap between theoretical research and practical application, making it an invaluable asset for advancing the Shift2DC initiative's goals. Its comprehensive features not only enhance technical understanding but also foster broader acceptance and integration of DC solutions across diverse sectors, thereby contributing significantly to the transition towards more sustainable energy systems.
