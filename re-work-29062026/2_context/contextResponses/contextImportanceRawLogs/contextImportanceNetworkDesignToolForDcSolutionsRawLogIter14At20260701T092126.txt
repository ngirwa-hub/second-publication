---

The Network Design Tool for DC solutions is critical in the Shift2DC project as it supports the design and optimization of complex DC grid configurations. By integrating static models of key devices within multiple ecosystem setups, this tool allows stakeholders to simulate various scenarios, enhancing decision-making processes related to infrastructure development and system integration. This capability is particularly important given the current lack of widespread direct user experience with medium- and low-voltage DC systems, as it helps bridge knowledge gaps and facilitates informed planning.

Furthermore, the ability to model different configurations aids in understanding how new technologies can be incorporated into existing grids or developed from scratch, thus promoting innovation and adoption. The tool's alignment with Current-OS and ODCA frameworks ensures compatibility and interoperability across various components, which is crucial for achieving a seamless transition to DC systems.

In summary, the Network Design Tool for DC solutions plays an indispensable role in advancing the objectives of the Shift2DC initiative by enabling detailed planning and optimization of DC infrastructure, thereby accelerating the adoption of sustainable energy technologies.
