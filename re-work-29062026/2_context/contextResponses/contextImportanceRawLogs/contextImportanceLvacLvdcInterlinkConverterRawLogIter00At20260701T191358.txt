The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a vital role in enabling the coexistence of current AC power systems with future DC-based solutions by effectively managing power flow between these two types of electrical architectures. This capability is essential for integrating renewable energy sources that often generate direct current, such as solar panels and certain wind turbines, into existing infrastructure without requiring extensive modifications or replacements.

In addition to its role in facilitating the integration of renewables, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter also supports a wide range of applications across data centers, residential buildings, industrial settings, and ports, which are highlighted as key demonstration areas in the Shift2DC project. By allowing these varied sectors to leverage both AC and DC technologies seamlessly, the converter enhances flexibility, efficiency, and sustainability in power management.

Moreover, the built-in droop control feature of the interlink converter reduces the complexity and cost associated with adding multiple downstream devices, which is particularly beneficial for scaling up DC architecture in practical scenarios. This simplification not only lowers installation and maintenance costs but also accelerates deployment timelines, addressing one of the major barriers to the adoption of DC solutions.

The converter's ability to operate effectively at low voltages aligns well with the project’s focus on medium- and low-voltage DC systems, which are increasingly relevant in urban environments where space constraints and safety considerations make them preferable. By facilitating the transition from AC to DC power distribution, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter supports the broader goals of reducing energy losses, improving grid stability, and enhancing overall system efficiency.

Furthermore, as industries move towards more sustainable practices, the ability to integrate renewable energy sources seamlessly into existing grids becomes increasingly important. The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter enables this integration by providing a reliable interface between AC and DC systems, thereby supporting the global shift towards cleaner energy solutions.

In summary, the importance of the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is underscored by its role in bridging current and future power systems, enhancing efficiency, reducing costs, and facilitating the adoption of renewable energy sources across various sectors. Its contribution to the Shift2DC project’s objectives makes it a critical component in advancing medium- and low-voltage DC technologies.
