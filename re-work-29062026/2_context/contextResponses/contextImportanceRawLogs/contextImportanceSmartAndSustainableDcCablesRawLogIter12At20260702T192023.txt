Smart and sustainable DC cables  
Rating: 4  
Justification: They ensure long-term reliability and ease of integration, crucial for the widespread adoption of DC systems.

---

This evaluation reflects the critical role that smart and sustainable DC cables play in facilitating a smooth transition to DC power distribution. Their design prioritizes durability and environmental sustainability, which are essential for meeting future energy demands while minimizing ecological impact. By mimicking standard AC cable handling, they simplify installation processes and reduce barriers to adoption across diverse demonstration sites. This adaptability is particularly important in the context of the Shift2DC initiative's goal to establish guidelines and roadmaps for DC solutions, as it directly supports the scalability and feasibility of medium- and low-voltage DC systems. The emphasis on ergonomic use and improved longevity also highlights their potential to enhance grid stability and efficiency, making them a cornerstone technology in advancing DC infrastructure.
