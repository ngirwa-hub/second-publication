The importance of smart and sustainable DC cables can be evaluated by considering their role in the successful deployment of DC technologies within various demonstration settings outlined in the Shift2DC project.

1. **Eco-Friendly Integration**: The emphasis on durable, environmentally friendly materials aligns with global sustainability goals. As renewable energy solutions become more prevalent, ensuring that infrastructure components like cables are sustainable is crucial for reducing environmental impact.

2. **Ease of Adoption**: By mimicking standard AC cable handling, these DC cables simplify the transition and adoption process across different demonstration sites. This ergonomic design consideration ensures that existing facilities can easily upgrade to or incorporate new DC systems without significant retraining or infrastructure changes.

3. **Reliability Across Demonstrations**: The cables are intended for use in diverse environments such as data centers, residential buildings, industrial setups, and ports. Their robustness and adaptability make them a cornerstone technology that supports the broader goals of each demonstration area by providing consistent performance across varied applications.

4. **Standardization and Interoperability**: Ensuring these cables can be easily integrated into multiple settings promotes standardization, which is critical for widespread DC adoption. This interoperability reduces costs and logistical challenges associated with custom solutions, facilitating a smoother transition to DC systems.

Given these factors, smart and sustainable DC cables are rated as "very important" (4) because they play an essential role in the practical implementation and success of the Shift2DC project's objectives across different sectors and demonstration sites.
