---

The smart and sustainable DC cables are rated as "very important" (rating of 4) because they play a foundational role in the widespread adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC solutions. Their design, which prioritizes durable and environmentally friendly materials, ensures that these cables can match or exceed the longevity of traditional AC cables. Additionally, by mimicking standard AC cable handling, they facilitate easier integration into existing systems across different demonstration sites such as data centers, buildings, industry, and ports. This ergonomic aspect reduces the learning curve for stakeholders involved in implementing DC technologies, thereby accelerating adoption rates and minimizing resistance to change.

Moreover, the environmental benefits of these cables contribute significantly to the broader goals of sustainability within the Shift2DC initiative. By utilizing materials that are less harmful to the environment, these cables help reduce the ecological footprint associated with deploying new infrastructure. This aligns well with global trends towards greener technologies and can enhance the public perception and acceptance of DC systems.

In summary, smart and sustainable DC cables are integral to the project's success due to their role in easing adoption barriers, ensuring long-term viability, and supporting environmental sustainability goals.
