This capability is essential as many current infrastructures are predominantly AC-based. By incorporating built-in droop control, this converter reduces the need for additional downstream devices, streamlining system complexity and enhancing efficiency. Its importance lies in its ability to support various application areas like data centers, buildings, industry, and ports by ensuring compatibility with existing systems while allowing the benefits of DC technology to be realized. This conversion capability is vital for widespread adoption and integration of DC solutions into current energy ecosystems.
