Solid-state circuit breaker  
Rating: 4  
Justification: Provides rapid fault detection and isolation, crucial for maintaining reliability in DC systems.

Solid-state circuit breakers are critical in medium- and low-voltage DC applications because they ensure safety and operational stability by quickly isolating faults. This capability is particularly important given the absence of natural current zero-crossing in DC, which makes traditional interruption methods less effective. The integration of real-time monitoring and IoT communication further enhances system management and efficiency, making solid-state circuit breakers a vital component for modern DC grids.
