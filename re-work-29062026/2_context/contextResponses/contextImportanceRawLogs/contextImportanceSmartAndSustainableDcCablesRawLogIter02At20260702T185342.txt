---

Smart and sustainable DC cables play a crucial role in the widespread adoption of DC technologies. Their design with durable and environmentally friendly materials ensures they can match or exceed the longevity of AC cables, which is essential for promoting sustainability. Furthermore, their ergonomic design that mimics standard AC cable handling simplifies integration across various demonstration sites, making them highly accessible and user-friendly. This ease of use is critical in facilitating broader adoption and reducing resistance to change from traditional AC systems. Therefore, these cables are considered very important within the context of the Shift2DC initiative.
