**Contextual Justification:** 

1. **Role in Integration**: In the context of integrating medium- and low-voltage DC systems into existing infrastructure (e.g., buildings, ports), connectors play a pivotal role by ensuring seamless connectivity between different components.

2. **Types of Connectors**: The project's focus on developing both passive and smart connectors highlights their importance. Passive connectors improve physical robustness, while smart connectors add functionalities like handling higher voltages and currents, which are essential for modern DC applications.

3. **Demonstration Sites**: Their implementation in buildings and potentially ports underscores their versatility and necessity across different environments where DC systems are being tested and deployed.

4. **Facilitating Adoption**: By mimicking standard AC cable handling, these connectors simplify the transition to DC technologies, making them more accessible and reducing resistance to change among users.

5. **Safety and Efficiency**: Enhanced safety features in smart connectors contribute significantly to the reliability of DC systems, which is crucial for gaining user trust and ensuring long-term sustainability of DC solutions.

Overall, the development and deployment of advanced DC connectors are vital to overcoming technical barriers and enabling broader acceptance and integration of DC technologies across various sectors.
