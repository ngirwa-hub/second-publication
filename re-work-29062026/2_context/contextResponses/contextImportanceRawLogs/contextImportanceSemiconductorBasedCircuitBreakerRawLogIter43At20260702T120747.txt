Rating: 4  
Justification: It ensures rapid fault interruption, crucial for safety and reliability in DC systems.

The semiconductor technology allows for faster response times, which enhances system protection against faults. Additionally, its compliance with Current/OS and ODCA standards ensures compatibility and interoperability across different devices and systems within a DC grid infrastructure.
