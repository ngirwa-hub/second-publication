This feature is particularly important given the regulatory barriers and infrastructure costs associated with DC technologies. By enabling detailed simulations and assessments, the tool helps identify potential cost savings and efficiency improvements, which are key factors in overcoming adoption barriers.

In addition to its design capabilities, the tool's alignment with industry standards ensures that the solutions developed are not only innovative but also practical for real-world applications. This standardization is vital for gaining regulatory approval and fostering trust among stakeholders, including those who may be hesitant due to mixed views in the literature about DC technologies. Overall, the DC solution design tool plays a pivotal role in advancing the Shift2DC project's goals by facilitating the seamless integration of new DC solutions into existing energy infrastructures.
