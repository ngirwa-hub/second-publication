The semiconductor-based circuit breaker is particularly vital as it addresses the unique challenge of interrupting faults in DC systems, where natural current zero-crossing does not occur as it does in AC systems. This capability is essential to maintain system integrity and prevent damage or hazards associated with prolonged fault conditions. By ensuring compliance with standards like Current/OS and ODCA, this solution also facilitates broader adoption by aligning with existing regulatory frameworks, thus supporting the integration of DC technologies into current infrastructure.
