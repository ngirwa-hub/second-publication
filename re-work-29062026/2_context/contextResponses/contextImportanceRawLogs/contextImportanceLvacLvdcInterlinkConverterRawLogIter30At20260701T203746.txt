The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a pivotal role in the Shift2DC initiative by enabling seamless interaction between conventional AC power systems and emerging DC grids. Its ability to incorporate built-in droop control reduces the complexity of downstream devices required for stable operation within the DC architecture, making it essential for efficient integration across various application areas like data centers, buildings, industry, and ports. The converter’s design supports the project's goal of creating a robust framework for adopting medium- and low-voltage DC solutions by minimizing compatibility issues with existing infrastructure. This is particularly important given the early stage of user engagement in DC technologies; facilitating easy integration can accelerate adoption and address potential barriers related to system complexity and cost.

Moreover, the interlink converter’s importance is underscored by its role in reducing transition costs for stakeholders who are currently reliant on AC systems but need to adopt DC solutions. By allowing a more gradual shift and supporting hybrid configurations, this technology reduces risks associated with large-scale infrastructure changes, thereby enhancing the economic viability of DC adoption. This aligns well with the project's aim of developing guidelines and roadmaps that address both technical challenges and market readiness for DC technologies.

Additionally, in the context of sustainability and environmental impact, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter contributes to more efficient energy use by optimizing power flow between AC and DC systems, which can lead to reduced energy losses and improved overall system performance. This is crucial for meeting regulatory requirements and societal expectations regarding energy efficiency and carbon footprint reduction.

In summary, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is a foundational technology within the Shift2DC project that not only facilitates technical integration but also supports broader strategic goals related to market adoption, economic feasibility, and environmental sustainability of DC systems.
