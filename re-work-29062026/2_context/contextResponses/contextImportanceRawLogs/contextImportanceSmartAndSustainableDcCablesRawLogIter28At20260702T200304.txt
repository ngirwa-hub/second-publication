Smart and sustainable DC cables  
Rating: 4  
Justification: They are crucial for enhancing adoption by ensuring durability, environmental friendliness, and ease of use.  

---

Smart and sustainable DC cables provide a foundation for the widespread implementation of medium- and low-voltage DC solutions. Their design focuses on using durable materials that are environmentally friendly, which is critical in promoting sustainability within energy systems. Additionally, these cables prioritize ergonomic handling, making them comparable to standard AC cables. This ease of use is vital for facilitating adoption across various demonstration sites, as it reduces the learning curve and potential resistance from users accustomed to traditional AC systems.

Moreover, by ensuring that these cables are both sustainable and user-friendly, they address one of the key barriers to adopting new technologies: the initial resistance due to unfamiliarity or perceived complexity. This aspect is particularly important in projects like Shift2DC, which aim to integrate DC solutions into diverse environments such as data centers, buildings, industry, and ports. The ability to seamlessly replace or complement existing infrastructure without significant retraining or adaptation underscores their importance.

In summary, the smart and sustainable design of these DC cables not only supports environmental goals but also plays a pivotal role in overcoming adoption barriers, making them very important for the success of medium- and low-voltage DC initiatives.
