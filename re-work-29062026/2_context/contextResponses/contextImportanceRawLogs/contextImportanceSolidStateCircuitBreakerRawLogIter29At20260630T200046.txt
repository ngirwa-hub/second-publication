The solid-state circuit breaker is a pivotal technology within the Shift2DC initiative due to its high-speed operation capability, which is essential for addressing the unique challenges posed by DC systems, such as the absence of natural current zero-crossing points. This makes it vital for protecting infrastructure from faults that can lead to equipment damage or fire hazards. Its integration with real-time monitoring and IoT communication further enhances grid management efficiency, making it a cornerstone technology in advancing medium- and low-voltage DC applications.
