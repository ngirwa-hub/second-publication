The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a significant role in bridging the gap between traditional AC power grids and emerging DC microgrids. As many existing buildings and industries are primarily designed around AC systems, transitioning to DC can be challenging without effective conversion solutions. The interlink converter enables seamless integration by converting low-voltage AC to DC, thus allowing for the coexistence of both systems during a transitional phase.

Furthermore, the built-in droop control feature reduces the need for additional downstream devices, simplifying system design and reducing costs associated with full-scale conversions. This makes it an attractive solution not only technically but also economically, as it lowers barriers to adoption by minimizing additional infrastructure investments. 

The converter's importance is heightened in applications where DC benefits are clear—such as energy efficiency improvements in data centers or renewable integration—but the AC legacy systems remain prevalent. By providing a practical path forward, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter supports incremental adoption and scalability of DC technologies across various sectors, aligning with the goals of the Shift2DC initiative to foster widespread acceptance and implementation of medium- and low-voltage DC solutions.
