---

The smart and sustainable DC cables play a crucial role in the Shift2DC initiative by providing an essential infrastructure component that supports the widespread adoption of DC solutions. Their importance is underscored by their design to ensure comparable or improved longevity relative to AC cables while being made from durable, environmentally friendly materials. This aspect not only aligns with sustainability goals but also addresses practical concerns regarding the lifespan and performance of DC systems.

Moreover, these cables prioritize ergonomic use, mimicking standard AC cable handling, which significantly simplifies adoption across various demonstration sites, including data centers, buildings, industry sectors, and ports. By facilitating ease of installation and integration into existing infrastructure, they lower barriers to entry for adopting DC technologies, making them a critical enabler in the transition towards more sustainable energy systems.

The emphasis on smart functionalities within these cables further enhances their importance by potentially integrating advanced monitoring and control capabilities directly into the cable system itself. This could lead to improved efficiency, safety, and reliability of DC grids, contributing significantly to the overall goals of the Shift2DC project. Therefore, considering their foundational role in enabling the practical and sustainable implementation of DC solutions across diverse applications, smart and sustainable DC cables are rated as "Very important" within the context of this initiative.
