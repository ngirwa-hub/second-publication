3 - Feasible

This rating reflects an overall assessment that DC solutions within the Shift2DC project are likely to be successfully integrated across target sectors such as data centers, buildings, industry, and ports. European efforts in standardization and deployment of LV/MV-DC microgrids contribute positively to this feasibility. The combination of advanced technologies like smart cables, connectors, protection systems, converters, measurement devices, design tools, network simulation tools, and solid-state circuit breakers indicates a strong potential for effective implementation. However, challenges such as interoperability, regulatory barriers, and infrastructure costs may prevent the solutions from being considered "very feasible" at this stage, thus warranting a rating of "feasible."
