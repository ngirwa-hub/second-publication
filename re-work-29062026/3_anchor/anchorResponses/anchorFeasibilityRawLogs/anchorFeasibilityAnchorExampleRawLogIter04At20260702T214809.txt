--- phi4-normative ---
3 - Feasible

This rating considers the promising aspects of DC solutions in medium- and low-voltage applications, acknowledging that while there are technical, regulatory, and societal challenges to overcome, the targeted sectors (data centers, buildings, industry, ports) present viable opportunities for integration. The development of advanced technologies like smart cables, converters, and protection systems, alongside supportive policy frameworks and growing interest in renewable energy integration, suggests a feasible path forward. However, given that the project is still early with limited end-user engagement and facing potential barriers to adoption such as costs, risks, and time constraints, a very high feasibility rating (4) would be premature. Therefore, a rating of 3 reflects a balanced view, recognizing both the substantial groundwork laid by the Shift2DC initiative and the uncertainties that remain in realizing widespread adoption across these sectors.
