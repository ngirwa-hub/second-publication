DC technologies face challenges with traditional AC breakers due to the lack of natural current zero-crossing, which makes interrupting faults more complex. Solid-state circuit breakers address this by providing high-speed interruption capabilities in microseconds, crucial for maintaining system stability and preventing damage. Their ability to handle real-time monitoring and IoT-enabled communication further integrates them into smart grid architectures, supporting efficient management and operation. Given the projected improvements in distribution efficiency with DC microgrids, particularly those incorporating photovoltaic (PV) systems and storage solutions, the role of advanced protective devices like solid-state circuit breakers becomes increasingly critical to maximize these benefits while ensuring safety and reliability.
