---

Smart and sustainable DC cables offer a significant advancement over traditional AC cables by addressing both environmental concerns and user ergonomics. By utilizing durable materials that minimize ecological impact while maintaining or improving longevity, these cables can substantially reduce the lifecycle costs associated with cable replacement and maintenance in DC microgrids. Their design also simplifies adoption across various demonstration sites, making them a critical enabler for widespread implementation of medium- and low-voltage DC systems.

The ergonomic handling aspect is particularly noteworthy, as it lowers the barrier to entry for technicians and engineers accustomed to AC systems, thereby accelerating training and reducing errors during installation and maintenance. This ease of use can lead to more efficient deployment and operation of DC grids, contributing to the overall efficiency gains reported in studies (2–6% improvement). Furthermore, by ensuring compatibility with existing infrastructure practices, smart and sustainable DC cables help mitigate one of the significant barriers to adoption: the perceived complexity and unfamiliarity of new technologies.

In summary, smart and sustainable DC cables are pivotal not only for their direct impact on system efficiency but also for their role in facilitating broader acceptance and integration of DC solutions. Their importance is underscored by their potential to enhance both technical performance and user experience, making them a cornerstone of the Shift2DC initiative's efforts to promote medium- and low-voltage DC adoption.
