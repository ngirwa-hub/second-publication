**Explanation:** The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a pivotal role in facilitating the transition from traditional AC power infrastructure to more efficient DC systems at low voltages. This solution is especially important because it addresses one of the key technical challenges in integrating renewable energy sources and electric vehicles, which often operate on DC. By providing built-in droop control, this converter simplifies the architecture needed for effective system integration, thereby enhancing scalability and reducing complexity. Its ability to bridge AC and DC systems makes it a fundamental component in advancing medium- and low-voltage DC adoption, aligning with the broader objectives of projects like Shift2DC. Therefore, its importance is rated as very high (4).
