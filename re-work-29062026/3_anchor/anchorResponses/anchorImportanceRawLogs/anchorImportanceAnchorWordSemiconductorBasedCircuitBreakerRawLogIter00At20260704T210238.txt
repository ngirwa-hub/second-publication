The semiconductor-based circuit breaker is pivotal in DC systems due to its ability to handle the lack of natural current zero-crossing present in AC systems, which makes interrupting faults challenging. This technology's development aligns with European efforts towards standardization and deployment of low-voltage (LV) and medium-voltage (MV) DC microgrids. Its importance is underscored by the need for safety and reliability across different sectors like manufacturing, data centers, and ports where DC systems are increasingly being adopted. By ensuring rapid fault interruption, it enhances the operational stability and efficiency of these microgrids, making it a cornerstone technology in advancing DC infrastructure.
