LVAC-LVDC Interlink Converter  
Rating: 4  
Justification: It is crucial for integrating existing AC infrastructure with new DC systems, facilitating the transition and maximizing efficiency improvements.  

The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a vital role in enabling seamless integration between AC and DC power systems. This function is particularly important as it allows for efficient utilization of both types of infrastructures without necessitating complete overhauls or replacements, which can be costly and time-consuming. By incorporating built-in droop control, the converter reduces the need for additional devices downstream, simplifying system architecture and potentially lowering costs.

In terms of efficiency improvements, DC microgrids are known to offer distribution-efficiency benefits ranging from 2% to 6%, even under efficient baselines. The interlink converter is instrumental in realizing these gains by facilitating effective power flow between the AC and DC domains. This capability ensures that renewable energy sources, often integrated into DC systems for better efficiency and reduced losses, can be effectively harnessed alongside existing AC-powered infrastructure.

Moreover, as DC technology adoption grows, particularly in sectors like data centers, buildings, industry, and ports—as highlighted by the Shift2DC project—the ability to bridge these two power systems becomes increasingly important. The interlink converter supports this transition by providing a reliable and efficient means of connecting disparate systems, thus addressing one of the significant barriers to widespread DC adoption: interoperability with existing AC grids.

In summary, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is rated as "Very Important" due to its critical role in facilitating the integration of AC and DC systems, supporting efficiency improvements, and enabling broader adoption of DC technologies.
