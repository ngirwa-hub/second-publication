By facilitating electrical sizing and supporting both Current-OS and ODCA frameworks, it addresses critical interoperability challenges that can otherwise hinder widespread adoption. By enabling accurate modeling of technologies and configurations, the tool helps identify potential efficiencies and cost savings, aligning with the project's goal to improve distribution efficiency by 2–6%.

Additionally, the tool's capability for techno-economic analysis aids stakeholders in making informed decisions regarding investments in DC infrastructure. By providing a clear understanding of economic implications alongside technical feasibility, the DC solution design tool plays a crucial role in advancing the project's objectives.

In summary, the DC solution design tool is essential for enabling effective planning, implementation, and optimization of DC systems within the Shift2DC initiative. Its comprehensive features support the project's aim to enhance distribution efficiency and facilitate the integration of renewables and other technologies into DC grids, making it a very important component of the overall strategy.
