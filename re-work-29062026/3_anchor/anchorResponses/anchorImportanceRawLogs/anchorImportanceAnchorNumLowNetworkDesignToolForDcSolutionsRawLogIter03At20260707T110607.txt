In the context of the Shift2DC project, where multiple demonstrators are being planned across diverse application areas (data centers, buildings, industry, ports), the ability to simulate and analyze network designs beforehand can significantly reduce trial-and-error in real-world implementations. This not only accelerates the adoption process by addressing potential technical challenges early but also aids in demonstrating the feasibility and benefits of DC solutions to stakeholders, including those concerned with efficiency improvements (2–6% as reported). Furthermore, a robust design tool aligns well with industry standards like Current/OS and ODCA, ensuring that the developed DC systems are both innovative and compliant.

Moreover, as the project aims to establish guidelines and roadmaps for widespread adoption of DC solutions, having a comprehensive network design tool becomes indispensable. It aids in bridging the gap between theoretical research and practical applications by providing actionable insights into system behavior under various operational scenarios. This capability is particularly important for addressing interoperability challenges and regulatory barriers that are often cited as significant obstacles to the adoption of new technologies. Thus, the network design tool not only supports technical development but also plays a pivotal role in facilitating broader acceptance and implementation of DC solutions across different sectors.  

In summary, the importance of the Network design tool for DC solutions is underscored by its comprehensive capabilities that support the entire lifecycle of DC system development—from conceptualization through to deployment and optimization—making it a cornerstone technology within the Shift2DC initiative.
