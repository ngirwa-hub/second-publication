The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a pivotal role in the transition to DC microgrids by enabling seamless integration between traditional AC power grids and emerging low-voltage DC (LVDC) networks. This conversion capability is essential for leveraging the advantages of DC, such as improved efficiency in distribution systems with PV and storage, as indicated by studies reporting 10–18% efficiency improvements. By reducing the need for multiple downstream devices through built-in droop control, this converter simplifies system architecture, enhancing reliability and cost-effectiveness.

In scenarios like data centers, buildings, industry, and ports—where DC adoption is being actively demonstrated—the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter ensures that existing AC infrastructure can coexist with new DC components. This interoperability supports gradual transitions without significant overhauls of current systems, addressing one of the major barriers to widespread DC implementation: compatibility with legacy equipment.

Furthermore, as part of a broader initiative like Shift2DC, which aims to establish comprehensive roadmaps and guidelines for DC adoption, having efficient conversion technologies is indispensable. It not only aids in immediate practical applications but also aligns with long-term strategic goals by facilitating the integration of renewable energy sources, thereby supporting sustainability objectives. Thus, its importance is underscored by both its technical necessity and alignment with broader industry trends towards greener, more efficient power systems.
