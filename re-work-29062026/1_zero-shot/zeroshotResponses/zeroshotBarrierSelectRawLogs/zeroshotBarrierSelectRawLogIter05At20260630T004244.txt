1. Lack of use-cases in which DC is advantageous - Addressing this barrier involves identifying and promoting specific applications where DC technology provides clear advantages over traditional AC systems, helping drive adoption and market growth.

3. Lack of trained personnel in DC systems - As with any new technology, there's a need for skilled professionals who can design, install, operate, and maintain DC systems effectively. Developing training programs and promoting awareness among stakeholders could help overcome this barrier.
4. High costs of DC solutions - To address the high cost issue, efforts should focus on reducing production costs through economies of scale, fostering research and development to improve efficiency, and exploring new business models that can make DC solutions more affordable for consumers.
5. Uncertain regulatory roadmap - Clear, consistent, and supportive regulations are necessary to create a favorable environment for DC technology adoption. Ensuring policymakers have access to accurate information about the benefits of DC systems and promoting dialogue between stakeholders can help clarify any uncertainty regarding the regulatory landscape.
