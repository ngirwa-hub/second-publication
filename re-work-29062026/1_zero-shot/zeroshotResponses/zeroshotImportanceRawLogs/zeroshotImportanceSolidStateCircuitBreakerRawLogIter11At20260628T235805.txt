Justification: The adoption of solid-state circuit breakers (SSCBs) in DC power systems is crucial for achieving reliable and efficient operation. SSCBs are essential for protecting against overcurrents, which can damage equipment and lead to system failures. They also offer superior performance compared to traditional electro-mechanical circuit breakers, as they do not have any moving parts that could wear out or malfunction. The integration of SSCBs in DC power systems promotes the reliability and longevity of these systems, which is particularly important for critical applications such as data centers and industrial processes.
