DC Solution Name: Smart and sustainable DC cables
Rating: 4
Justification: The use of smart and sustainable DC cables is crucial in the transition to renewable energy sources, as it enables efficient energy transmission with reduced environmental impact. These cables are designed for high-voltage direct current (HVDC) systems that can transport large amounts of clean energy over long distances while maintaining low losses and minimizing the need for transformers. This leads to a more sustainable and resilient power grid, enabling integration of variable renewable energy sources such as solar and wind power. Furthermore, smart features in these cables enable remote monitoring, control, and diagnostics, improving system reliability and reducing maintenance costs.
