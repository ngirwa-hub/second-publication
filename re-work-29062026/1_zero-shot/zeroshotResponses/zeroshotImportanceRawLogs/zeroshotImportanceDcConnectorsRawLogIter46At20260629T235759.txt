--- 

In the context of the Shift2DC project, which aims to promote DC technologies across diverse sectors like ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, DC connectors play a pivotal role. They enable efficient and reliable connections between different components of DC power systems, facilitating smooth energy transfer and enhancing system compatibility. This interoperability is essential for maximizing the benefits of DC integration, such as improved energy efficiency, reduced conversion losses, and simplified infrastructure. Moreover, standardized DC connectors can help overcome regulatory barriers by ensuring compliance with industry standards, thus accelerating the adoption of DC technologies. Therefore, their importance in achieving the objectives of the Shift2DC project cannot be overstated.
