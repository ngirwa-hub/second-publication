---

DC connectors facilitate the seamless transfer of direct current between devices, reducing energy losses that occur during AC-DC conversion. This efficiency is particularly vital in environments like ports, industry, data centers, and buildings where large volumes of power are managed. By minimizing conversion stages, DC connectors contribute significantly to energy savings and operational reliability.

Moreover, standardized DC connectors enhance interoperability among different equipment and manufacturers, promoting broader adoption of DC technologies. This standardization can lead to reduced costs and increased innovation in the development of new DC-powered solutions. Furthermore, the safety aspect is paramount; well-designed DC connectors ensure secure connections that prevent electrical hazards, thereby supporting safe operation across all sectors.

In summary, DC connectors are a foundational component for the successful implementation and widespread adoption of DC technologies, making them very important within the Shift2DC project's objectives.
