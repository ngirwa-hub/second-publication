Network Design Tool for DC Solutions  
Rating: 4  
Justification: It is crucial for optimizing infrastructure layout and ensuring efficient integration of DC systems.  

The network design tool plays a pivotal role in the Shift2DC project by enabling precise planning and optimization of DC power distribution networks, which are critical for maximizing efficiency and reliability across ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. This tool aids in addressing engineering challenges such as interoperability with existing AC infrastructure, regulatory compliance, and cost-effective deployment strategies. By simulating various scenarios and configurations, the network design tool helps stakeholders visualize potential outcomes and make informed decisions about system integration, thereby facilitating a smoother transition to DC power systems. Additionally, it supports innovation by allowing for the exploration of new technologies and methodologies in high-voltage DC transmission and low-voltage DC grids, ultimately contributing to more sustainable and resilient energy solutions.
