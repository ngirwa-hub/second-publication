--- 

Static protection systems are vital in environments prone to high levels of electrical disturbances, such as ports, industries, data centers, and buildings, which are highlighted by the Shift2DC project. These systems protect equipment from damage caused by lightning strikes or static electricity, ensuring operational continuity and safety. In DC power applications, where sensitive electronics and high-voltage components may be present, the risk of electrical disturbances can lead to costly downtimes or irreversible damage. Thus, a robust static protection system is fundamental for maintaining reliability and protecting investments in these sectors.
