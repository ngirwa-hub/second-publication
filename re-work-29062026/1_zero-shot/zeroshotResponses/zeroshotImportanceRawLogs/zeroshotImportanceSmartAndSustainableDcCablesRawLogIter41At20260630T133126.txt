---

In the context of promoting DC technologies as per the Shift2DC project, smart and sustainable DC cables are vital due to their ability to minimize energy loss over long distances compared to traditional AC systems. This reduction is essential for integrating renewable sources efficiently, especially where they are often located far from consumption centers. Moreover, these cables support the sustainability goals by being designed with materials and technologies that have a lower environmental impact throughout their lifecycle. Thus, in both ports, industrial settings, data centers, and buildings, smart and sustainable DC cables play an instrumental role in achieving energy efficiency and reducing carbon footprints.
