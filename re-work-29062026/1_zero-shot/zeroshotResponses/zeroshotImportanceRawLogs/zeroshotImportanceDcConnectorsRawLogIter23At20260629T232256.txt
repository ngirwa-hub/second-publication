DC Connectors  
Rating: 4  
Justification: DC connectors are essential for ensuring safe and efficient energy transfer, critical in the deployment of DC technologies across various sectors.  

---

The Shift2DC project focuses on implementing DC solutions to enhance efficiency and sustainability in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. DC connectors play a pivotal role in this context by facilitating seamless integration and interoperability of DC systems, which is crucial for their widespread adoption and effectiveness. By providing reliable connections that minimize energy losses during transmission and conversion, DC connectors help maximize the benefits of using direct current technologies. Additionally, they support the scalability and modularity needed to adapt to different applications and future expansions within these sectors.
