**Detailed Justification:**

1. **Reliability and Safety:** Solid-state circuit breakers provide fast response times to overcurrent conditions without the mechanical wear associated with traditional electromechanical breakers. This is particularly important in environments where reliability and safety are paramount, such as ports handling heavy machinery or data centers requiring uninterrupted power supply.

2. **Efficiency:** These devices operate with high efficiency and minimal energy loss, which is vital for optimizing energy use in DC grids integrated with renewable sources like solar panels or wind turbines.

3. **Scalability and Flexibility:** Solid-state circuit breakers can be easily scaled and adapted to different voltage levels and configurations, making them suitable for diverse applications within ports, industries, data centers, and buildings as promoted by the Shift2DC project.

4. **Integration with Renewable Energy:** As renewable energy sources become more prevalent, the ability to manage power flow efficiently and safely becomes critical. Solid-state circuit breakers facilitate seamless integration of these sources into existing infrastructure.

5. **Protection Against Faults:** They offer superior protection against electrical faults, reducing downtime and maintenance costs, which is crucial for high-stakes environments like industrial settings and data centers.

Overall, the adoption of solid-state circuit breakers aligns with the goals of the Shift2DC project by enhancing the performance and resilience of DC power systems in various applications.
