---

Explanation:

1. **Efficiency**: Smart DC cables enhance the efficiency of power transmission by reducing resistive losses, which is especially important in high-power applications such as industrial settings and data centers.

2. **Sustainability**: These cables are designed to be more sustainable through materials that have a lower environmental impact or longer life spans, aligning with the goals of renewable energy integration.

3. **Smart Features**: Integration of sensors and monitoring capabilities allows for real-time data on cable performance, enabling proactive maintenance and reducing downtime.

4. **Application Relevance**: In ports and industrial settings, where power demands are high and reliability is critical, these cables ensure continuous operation without interruptions due to power issues.

5. **Innovation in Infrastructure**: As part of the Shift2DC project, they represent a step forward in modernizing infrastructure to accommodate DC technologies more effectively, supporting broader adoption and interoperability.
