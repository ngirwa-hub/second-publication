Rating: 4  
Justification: Essential for protecting DC infrastructure from lightning and electrostatic discharge, ensuring system reliability and safety.  

In contexts like ports and data centers involved in the Shift2DC project, static protection systems are crucial due to their exposure to environmental factors that can induce high-voltage transients. These systems safeguard sensitive equipment and maintain operational continuity by dissipating or redirecting static charges safely. Their importance is underscored by the need for uninterrupted power supply and protection of assets in critical infrastructure settings, where failures could lead to significant financial losses and safety hazards.
