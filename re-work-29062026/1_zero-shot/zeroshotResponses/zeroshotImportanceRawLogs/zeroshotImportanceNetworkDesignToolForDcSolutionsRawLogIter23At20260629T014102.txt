1. **System Integration**: They help in seamlessly integrating diverse DC-powered systems, which is vital for reducing losses and maximizing efficiency.
   
2. **Optimization**: These tools allow for the optimization of power flows, minimizing transmission losses and enhancing overall system performance.
   
3. **Scalability**: Design tools support scalable solutions that can grow with increasing demands from expanding infrastructure projects like ports and data centers.

4. **Interoperability Assessment**: They aid in assessing interoperability issues, a significant challenge given the diverse technologies involved in DC power systems.

5. **Regulatory Compliance**: Ensuring designs meet regulatory standards is streamlined by using sophisticated modeling capabilities provided by network design tools.
