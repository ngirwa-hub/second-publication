The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is a crucial component for the adoption of DC technologies across various sectors like ports, industry, data centers, and buildings as promoted by the Shift2DC project. By enabling seamless conversion from low-voltage alternating current (LVAC) to low-voltage direct current (LVDC), these converters play an essential role in optimizing energy efficiency and reducing power losses associated with multiple conversions.

In environments such as ports and industrial sites where both AC and DC systems are prevalent, having efficient interlink converters ensures that the benefits of using DC technologies—such as lower transmission losses and improved compatibility with renewable energy sources—are fully realized. This capability is particularly important in data centers and buildings where energy efficiency directly translates to operational cost savings and reduced carbon footprints.

Furthermore, the integration of these converters supports the development of more resilient and flexible power infrastructures, capable of accommodating future technological advancements and increased electrification demands. Therefore, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is considered important for advancing DC technology adoption in the sectors targeted by the Shift2DC project.
