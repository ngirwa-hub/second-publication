The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is crucial because it allows for the efficient transition between low-voltage alternating current (LVAC) and low-voltage direct current (LVDC). This capability is particularly important as it enables existing infrastructure to integrate DC technologies without requiring complete overhauls. By doing so, it supports a more gradual and cost-effective adoption of DC systems in various sectors like ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, which are key areas targeted by the Shift2DC project. The converter's ability to enhance energy efficiency and reduce conversion losses makes it a vital component for modernizing energy systems while supporting sustainability goals.
