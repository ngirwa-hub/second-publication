Static Protection System  
Rating: 4  
Justification: Static protection is crucial for safeguarding sensitive DC equipment and infrastructure in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings from electrical surges.  

--- 

In the context of DC technologies promoted by the Shift2DC project, static protection systems are essential for maintaining operational integrity and safety. These systems prevent damage caused by electrostatic discharges (ESD), which can disrupt or destroy electronic components commonly used in these environments. Given the high reliance on electronics within data centers and industrial settings, ensuring their protection against ESD is vital for both efficiency and longevity of equipment. This makes static protection highly important in supporting the integration and reliability of DC systems across diverse applications.
