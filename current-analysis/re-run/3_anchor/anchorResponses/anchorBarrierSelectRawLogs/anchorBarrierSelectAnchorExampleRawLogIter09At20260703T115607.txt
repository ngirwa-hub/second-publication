These five barriers represent significant challenges for the widespread adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. Power losses, quality, and safety issues are crucial concerns due to the potential impact on efficiency and system performance. Reduced reliability in DC devices can lead to operational disruptions and increased maintenance costs. Uncertain utility interaction is a major concern as it can affect net metering policies, utility ownership models, and agreed standards. Lack of pilot projects is a hindrance to demonstrating the practicality and viability of DC technologies for various applications. Lastly, public perception plays a crucial role in the acceptance and championing of DC installations, which can impact their adoption rates.
