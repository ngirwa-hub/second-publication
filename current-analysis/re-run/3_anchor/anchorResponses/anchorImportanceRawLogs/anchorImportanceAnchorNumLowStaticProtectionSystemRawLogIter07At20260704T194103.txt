**Additional Contextual Justification:**

The integration of a static protection system is crucial given the inherent differences between AC and DC systems. In DC grids, there is no natural zero-crossing point to help extinguish arcs during faults, making traditional fault management techniques less effective. This necessitates ultra-fast protection devices capable of rapidly isolating faults to prevent potential damage or hazards.

Moreover, as the adoption of DC microgrids grows, particularly in applications like edge data centers and industrial settings where reliability is paramount, having robust protection systems becomes even more essential. These systems not only enhance safety but also improve overall grid efficiency by minimizing downtime and maintenance costs associated with fault occurrences.

The static protection system's ability to quickly identify and isolate faults aligns well with the goals of improving distribution-efficiency in DC microgrids, as highlighted in studies reporting a 2–6% improvement. This efficiency gain is partly due to reduced energy losses during fault conditions, underscoring the importance of advanced protection systems.

In summary, the static protection system's role in ensuring operational safety and reliability, coupled with its contribution to efficiency improvements, underscores its very important rating within the context of DC microgrid deployment and optimization.
