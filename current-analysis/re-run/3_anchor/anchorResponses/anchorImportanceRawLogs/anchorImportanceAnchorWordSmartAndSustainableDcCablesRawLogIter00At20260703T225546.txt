---

Smart and sustainable DC cables play a critical role in the widespread adoption of medium- and low-voltage direct current (LV/MV-DC) systems across various sectors, as indicated by ongoing standardization efforts and deployments throughout Europe. Their design prioritizes durability and environmental friendliness while maintaining or improving upon the longevity seen with traditional alternating current (AC) cables. This focus on sustainability aligns well with European regulatory frameworks that emphasize reducing carbon footprints and enhancing resource efficiency.

The ergonomic handling characteristics of these DC cables, designed to mimic standard AC cable management practices, facilitate smoother transitions for installations across demonstration sites. This compatibility is crucial in overcoming one of the significant barriers to adoption: the perceived complexity and unfamiliarity associated with transitioning from established AC systems to new DC solutions. By ensuring that these cables can be easily integrated into existing infrastructures without requiring extensive retraining or redesigns, they directly address both technical and social acceptance challenges.

Moreover, as European microgrids expand across sectors like residential buildings, industrial facilities, ports, and data centers, the robustness of these DC cables ensures reliability and performance consistency. This is particularly important in applications such as vehicle-to-grid (V2G) systems and distributed photovoltaic (DPV) setups, where stability and efficiency are paramount. By providing a foundational technology that supports the seamless operation of various advanced DC solutions—such as smart connectors, semiconductor-based circuit breakers, and solid-state circuit breakers—the smart and sustainable DC cables underpin the overall success of projects like Shift2DC.

In summary, given their alignment with sustainability goals, ease of integration, and critical support role in diverse applications within European microgrids, these cables are not just important but essential for realizing the vision of a robust, efficient, and environmentally friendly energy future. Therefore, they receive a rating of 4 on the importance scale, indicating their very high significance in the context of European LV/MV-DC deployments and standardization efforts.
