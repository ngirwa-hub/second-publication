By integrating static models of key devices, it supports multiple configurations within a DC ecosystem, which is essential for addressing distribution-efficiency improvements reported in other studies. This capability allows stakeholders to test various scenarios and identify optimal solutions before implementation, thereby reducing risks associated with system deployment and enhancing overall project success.

Moreover, the tool's ability to simulate diverse DC ecosystems helps bridge gaps between theoretical models and practical applications, making it invaluable for decision-makers aiming to adopt medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. Its importance is further underscored by its alignment with evolving standards like Current/OS and ODCA, ensuring that designs are both forward-compatible and compliant with industry best practices. This comprehensive functionality makes the Network design tool a cornerstone in advancing the Shift2DC initiative's objectives of widespread DC adoption.
