---

In the context of European deployments and standardization efforts focusing on workable low-voltage (LV) and medium-voltage (MV) direct current (DC) microgrids across various sectors, smart and sustainable DC cables are rated as "Very important" for several reasons:

1. **Sustainability**: The use of durable, environmentally friendly materials aligns with the European Union's strong emphasis on sustainability and environmental protection in energy projects.

2. **Ease of Adoption**: By mimicking standard AC cable handling, these cables facilitate smoother integration into existing infrastructures, which is essential for widespread adoption across different sectors such as ports, buildings, industry, and data centers.

3. **Longevity and Reliability**: Ensuring comparable or improved longevity relative to AC cables addresses one of the critical barriers in adopting new technologies—reliability over time—which is vital for building trust and confidence among stakeholders involved in these microgrid projects.

Overall, smart and sustainable DC cables play a foundational role in supporting the infrastructure needs of LV/MV-DC microgrids, making them indispensable to the success of such initiatives across Europe.
