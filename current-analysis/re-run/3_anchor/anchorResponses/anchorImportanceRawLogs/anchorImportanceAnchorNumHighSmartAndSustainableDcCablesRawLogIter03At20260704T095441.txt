Smart and sustainable DC cables  
Rating: 4  
Justification: They are crucial for ensuring reliable, efficient distribution of power in DC systems with reduced environmental impact.  

The importance of Smart and sustainable DC cables is underscored by their role in facilitating the transition to medium- and low-voltage DC grids. These cables are designed to be durable and environmentally friendly, which aligns with global sustainability goals while maintaining or improving upon the longevity of traditional AC cables. Their ergonomic design simplifies adoption across various demonstration sites, reducing barriers related to handling and installation.

Incorporating smart technologies within these cables can significantly enhance grid performance by enabling better monitoring and control, crucial for managing renewable energy sources like solar panels integrated in DC microgrids. The ability to handle higher voltages and currents efficiently translates into improved distribution-efficiency, which is essential given the projected 10–18% efficiency improvements noted in DC microgrid scenarios with PV and storage.

Moreover, as DC systems become more widespread, especially in applications such as data centers, buildings, industrial sites, and ports, the demand for robust and sustainable infrastructure will increase. Smart and sustainable DC cables are pivotal in meeting these demands by providing a reliable backbone for these grids while minimizing ecological footprints, thus supporting broader adoption of DC technologies.
