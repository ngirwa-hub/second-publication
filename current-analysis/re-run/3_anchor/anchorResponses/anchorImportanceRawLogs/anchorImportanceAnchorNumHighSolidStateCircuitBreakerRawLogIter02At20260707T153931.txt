The solid-state circuit breaker is rated as "Very important" because it addresses a critical challenge in DC systems—lack of natural current zero-crossing—which conventional AC breakers rely on. This feature allows for ultra-fast interruption of faults, significantly improving the operational stability and safety of DC grids, which are increasingly integrated with renewable energy sources like PV systems. The ability to handle thermal and current stresses realistically further supports its importance in maintaining system integrity under varying load conditions. Additionally, real-time monitoring and IoT-enabled communication enhance grid management and fault response capabilities, making it a pivotal component for the advancement of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies.
