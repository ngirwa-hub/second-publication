In summary, smart and sustainable DC cables are a cornerstone technology for advancing medium- and low-voltage DC solutions. Their design principles directly address critical challenges such as sustainability, ease of adoption, and efficiency improvement, making them an indispensable part of the shift towards more sustainable and efficient energy systems.
