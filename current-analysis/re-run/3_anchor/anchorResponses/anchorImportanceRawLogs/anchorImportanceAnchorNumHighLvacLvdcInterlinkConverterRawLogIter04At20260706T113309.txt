The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a pivotal role in the transition from traditional AC systems to modern DC grids by enabling seamless power flow between the two types of electrical systems. Its importance is underscored by its ability to bridge legacy infrastructure with new DC applications, which is essential for maximizing distribution-efficiency improvements and achieving the 10–18% gains reported in DC microgrid scenarios incorporating photovoltaics (PV) and storage solutions.

By providing built-in droop control, this converter minimizes the need for additional downstream devices within a DC architecture, simplifying system design and reducing overall costs. This efficiency not only lowers installation expenses but also enhances operational reliability and performance, further encouraging adoption across various sectors such as data centers, industrial settings, and ports where integration challenges are prevalent.

Moreover, in applications like microgrids with PV and storage systems, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter ensures that energy generated from renewable sources can be effectively utilized within DC networks. This capability is particularly valuable for optimizing energy distribution and utilization, thereby supporting sustainability goals and reducing reliance on fossil fuels.

In summary, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter's ability to facilitate the integration of AC and DC systems, streamline system design through droop control, and enhance renewable energy utilization makes it a very important component in advancing medium- and low-voltage DC solutions.
