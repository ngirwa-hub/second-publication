---

The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a pivotal role in the transition to medium- and low-voltage DC grids by facilitating compatibility with established AC systems. This ensures that new DC technologies can be integrated into current infrastructures without requiring significant overhauls or replacements, thereby reducing costs and accelerating deployment. Its ability to include droop control further minimizes the need for additional devices downstream in a DC architecture, enhancing system efficiency and reliability. By addressing these integration challenges, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is instrumental in advancing the Shift2DC initiative's objectives.
