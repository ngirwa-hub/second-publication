The importance rating reflects the role of DC connectors in bridging current technologies with future DC deployments. As European projects demonstrate viable microgrids incorporating DC solutions, ensuring robust and versatile connection points is essential. Smart and passive DC connectors not only improve physical resilience but also enable advanced functionalities like high-voltage and current handling, thus supporting seamless integration into existing infrastructure. This adaptability makes them vital for sectors such as buildings, ports, and data centers where diverse applications and user interactions occur.

Moreover, the development of standardized DC connectors aligns with ongoing efforts in European standardization bodies (Current-OS, ODCA), which aim to harmonize technical specifications across member states. By facilitating consistent connectivity solutions, these connectors reduce complexity and cost barriers associated with transitioning from AC to DC systems. The inclusion of microelectronics within smart connectors further underscores their significance by enhancing system monitoring and control capabilities, essential for optimizing the performance and reliability of DC networks.

In summary, given their foundational role in ensuring compatibility, resilience, and advanced functionality across varied applications, DC connectors are rated as very important (4) in the context of European deployments and standardization efforts.
