The DC solution design tool is rated as very important (4) due to its critical role in enabling the efficient and effective design of DC systems. By providing a user-friendly interface that supports drag-and-drop usability and accommodates models for various DC technologies, it directly addresses one of the major challenges in DC system adoption: complexity and accessibility of design processes. The tool's alignment with both Current-OS and ODCA frameworks ensures compatibility and standardization across different applications, which is vital for interoperability—a key consideration for widespread DC technology implementation.

Moreover, its capability to support electrical sizing and techno-economic analysis empowers stakeholders to make informed decisions by evaluating the economic viability and technical feasibility of proposed DC solutions. This aspect is particularly important in lightening the financial and operational risks associated with adopting new technologies. In scenarios where distribution-efficiency improvements of 10–18% have been modeled, tools that aid in optimizing system design can significantly contribute to realizing these efficiencies at scale.

Given the early stage of the Shift2DC project and the focus on medium- and low-voltage DC systems, the ability to rapidly prototype and iterate on DC designs is invaluable. The DC solution design tool not only accelerates this process but also democratizes access to complex system modeling and simulation capabilities that were previously available only to specialists with advanced technical skills or expensive software licenses. This broad accessibility can catalyze innovation and adoption across diverse sectors, including those highlighted in the project such as data centers, buildings, industry, and ports.

In summary, the DC solution design tool is a cornerstone for advancing the Shift2DC initiative by addressing critical barriers to adoption, enhancing system efficiency, and fostering an ecosystem of interoperable and economically viable DC solutions. Its importance cannot be overstated, making it a very important (4) component of the project's technology portfolio.
