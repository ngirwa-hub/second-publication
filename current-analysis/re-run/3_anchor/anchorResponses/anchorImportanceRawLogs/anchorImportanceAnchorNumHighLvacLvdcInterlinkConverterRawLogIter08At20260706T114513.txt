This converter not only enables the direct utilization of renewable energy sources by facilitating their integration into existing AC infrastructure but also supports the realization of the inherent efficiency benefits associated with DC power distribution. By incorporating built-in droop control, it reduces the need for additional downstream devices within the DC architecture, streamlining system design and potentially lowering costs.

Moreover, in contexts such as data centers, buildings, industry, and ports—where the Shift2DC project aims to demonstrate the viability of DC solutions—the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter serves as a critical enabler. It allows these sectors to transition more smoothly to DC power systems, overcoming one of the significant technical barriers: the compatibility between legacy AC infrastructure and new DC technologies.
