Smart and sustainable DC cables  
Rating: 4  
Justification: They are critical for ensuring long-term, eco-friendly integration of LV/MV-DC microgrids across various sectors.  

---  

Explanation: Smart and sustainable DC cables play a pivotal role in the deployment of low-voltage (LV) and medium-voltage (MV) direct current (DC) microgrids by providing reliable and environmentally friendly options for power transmission. Their design, which mirrors that of standard alternating current (AC) cables while offering improved durability and sustainability, facilitates easier adoption and integration into existing infrastructure. This is particularly important in European contexts where there are active efforts to standardize and deploy workable LV/MV-DC microgrids across different sectors such as residential, industrial, and port environments. Moreover, the focus on sustainable materials aligns with broader environmental goals, making these cables not only functionally significant but also socially and environmentally beneficial.
