The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a pivotal role in bridging the gap between traditional AC power systems and emerging DC solutions. By allowing for efficient conversion and compatibility between these two types of electrical systems, it addresses one of the significant barriers to widespread DC adoption: interoperability with existing infrastructure. This capability is especially important given that many existing buildings and industrial facilities are designed around AC technology.

Moreover, the converter's built-in droop control feature reduces the need for additional downstream devices in a DC architecture, simplifying system design and potentially lowering costs. This aspect enhances its importance by contributing to more straightforward implementation processes and reducing technical complexity for end-users.

In summary, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is very important because it facilitates the integration of DC technologies into existing AC-dominated environments, thereby promoting broader acceptance and utilization of DC solutions across various sectors.
