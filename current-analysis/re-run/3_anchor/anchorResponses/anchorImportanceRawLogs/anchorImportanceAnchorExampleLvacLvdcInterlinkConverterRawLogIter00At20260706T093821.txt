---

The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a pivotal role in bridging the gap between traditional AC power systems and emerging DC solutions. Its ability to facilitate this transition is essential for enabling widespread adoption of DC technologies in medium- and low-voltage applications. By incorporating droop control, it reduces the need for additional devices downstream, enhancing system efficiency and simplifying design complexities.

In the context of the Shift2DC project, which aims to establish guidelines and roadmaps for DC solutions, the interlink converter's importance is underscored by its potential to support diverse demonstration sites, including buildings, data centers, and ports. Its functionality aligns with the project's goals of integrating renewable energy sources and improving grid performance through better power flow management.

Moreover, as the project explores various applications such as powering edge data centers and residential buildings with DC solutions, the interlink converter becomes a critical component in ensuring compatibility and reliability across different systems. This makes it an important asset in advancing the objectives of Shift2DC, particularly in facilitating smooth transitions and enhancing the scalability of DC technologies.
