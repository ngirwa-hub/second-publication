Based on my expertise, I would select the following five barriers for further discussion:

1. Power losses, quality, and safety issues (Barrier 1) - These concerns are significant as they directly impact the efficiency and reliability of DC systems. Addressing these challenges can help improve the overall performance and cost-effectiveness of DC technologies.
2. Reduced reliability in DC devices (Barrier 2) - Enhancing the dependability of DC components is crucial for widespread adoption, as it ensures the longevity and robustness of DC systems.
3. Lack of use-cases in which DC is advantageous (Barrier 3) - Identifying and showcasing compelling applications that demonstrate the benefits of DC technology can help drive its adoption among various sectors.
4. Uncertain utility interaction (net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards) (Barrier 4) - Clarifying regulations and establishing clear guidelines for integrating DC systems with existing electricity infrastructure is essential to facilitate their deployment.
5. Lack of pilot projects (Barrier 5) - Implementing pilot projects can help validate the feasibility and performance of DC technologies, thereby fostering confidence among potential adopters and investors.
