--- phi4-normative ---
3 - Feasible

This rating reflects a positive outlook towards the integration of DC solutions across various sectors outlined in the Shift2DC project, considering the comprehensive range of technologies developed and their alignment with current energy and sustainability goals. While challenges remain, particularly around user adoption and infrastructure adaptation, the strategic focus on demonstrating these technologies in practical settings (data centers, buildings, industry, ports) suggests a strong potential for feasibility. The inclusion of innovative solutions such as smart DC cables, converters, protection systems, and design tools indicates a forward-thinking approach that addresses both technical and operational aspects of DC system integration. Moreover, the project's engagement with experts across different domains to identify barriers and opportunities further supports the feasibility assessment by ensuring that diverse perspectives are considered in shaping these technologies for real-world applications.

The emphasis on developing enablers like software tools and IT solution architectures suggests a robust foundation for supporting the adoption and scaling of DC solutions. Demonstrators planned in key application areas not only provide tangible proof-of-concept but also facilitate learning and adaptation, which are critical for overcoming sector-specific challenges and enhancing system interoperability. The focus on renewable integration, particularly through initiatives like vehicle-to-grid (V2G) systems and photovoltaic (PV) installations, aligns with global trends towards decarbonization and energy efficiency, thereby increasing the societal and environmental relevance of these solutions.

Furthermore, the project's effort to include feasibility studies and create DC roadmaps based on demonstrator outcomes indicates a comprehensive approach to addressing both current and future needs. This strategic planning is essential for navigating the complexities of transitioning to DC systems in existing infrastructures and markets characterized by entrenched AC norms.

However, while the project showcases significant promise, its early stage means that some uncertainties remain regarding widespread acceptance, regulatory support, and cost-effectiveness at scale. These factors could impact the speed and extent of adoption across different sectors. Thus, while highly feasible in certain contexts—especially those with a strong emphasis on innovation and sustainability—the overall feasibility is cautiously optimistic, acknowledging both the potential and challenges ahead.

In conclusion, rating the feasibility as "3 - Feasible" captures an encouraging perspective on the project's ambitions to advance DC technologies within targeted sectors. It reflects confidence in the technical solutions developed, their alignment with energy transition goals, and the strategic approach of the Shift2DC project while remaining mindful of the hurdles that lie ahead in realizing widespread implementation.
