--- phi4-normative ---
3 - Feasible

The Shift2DC project's DC solutions are designed with a strong focus on integration, innovation, and addressing current technological gaps in medium- and low-voltage applications. The involvement of experts from diverse sectors such as ports, buildings, manufacturing, and utilities suggests a comprehensive approach to tackling the challenges associated with DC adoption. The inclusion of demonstrators across various application areas like data centers, buildings, industry, and ports indicates practical testing environments that can validate these technologies in real-world settings.

The solutions themselves are well-rounded, addressing critical components such as smart cables, connectors, protection systems, converters, design tools, and measurement devices. These components are fundamental for ensuring the reliability, efficiency, and safety of DC grids. The project's emphasis on developing tools that align with current standards (Current/OS, ODCA) further supports their feasibility by facilitating easier integration into existing infrastructure.

Moreover, the focus on renewable energy integration, such as solar panels in data centers and ports, enhances the sustainability aspect of these solutions, which is increasingly important for future-proofing energy systems. The inclusion of technologies like V2G chargers also demonstrates a forward-thinking approach to maximizing resource utilization.

While there are inherent challenges in transitioning from traditional AC systems to DC grids, such as infrastructure compatibility and user acceptance, the project's structured methodology and stakeholder engagement suggest that these hurdles can be effectively managed. Therefore, considering the comprehensive nature of the solutions and their alignment with industry needs and standards, the overall feasibility of implementing DC solutions in the target sectors is deemed feasible.

This rating reflects a comprehensive evaluation of the Shift2DC project's DC solutions, considering their potential integration across various target sectors such as data centers, buildings, industry, and ports. The diverse range of technologies—from smart cables to advanced semiconductor-based circuit breakers—demonstrates a strong foundation for enabling medium- and low-voltage DC adoption. However, challenges related to interoperability, regulatory barriers, and infrastructure costs are inherent in pioneering new power systems. These factors contribute to the assessment that while feasible, achieving widespread implementation will require overcoming significant technical and systemic hurdles. The expert elicitation process further supports this rating by highlighting both opportunities and uncertainties within the literature, indicating a balanced feasibility outlook across the target sectors.
