--- phi4-subject-matter ---
3 - Feasible

This rating reflects an assessment of the overall feasibility of implementing DC solutions in the target sectors (data centers, buildings, industry, and ports) as described in the Shift2DC project. The project incorporates advanced technologies, such as smart cables, converters, and protection systems, along with demonstrator plans across various applications. These initiatives suggest a strong foundation for the integration and adoption of DC power solutions within these sectors, supported by expert insights and planned demonstrations that address both technical challenges and practical implementation scenarios. However, given the early stage of engagement with end users and the complexity inherent in transitioning to new power systems, there remain uncertainties and hurdles that must be overcome before reaching a "very feasible" status. Therefore, while promising, the solutions are currently assessed as feasible rather than very feasible due to these considerations.
