--- phi4-generalist ---
3 - Feasible

This rating reflects an assessment that, based on the current stage of development and planned demonstrations in various sectors (data centers, buildings, industry, ports), DC solutions within the Shift2DC project are generally feasible. The initiative is actively addressing integration challenges and developing necessary technologies to enhance adoption across these areas. While there are still uncertainties and barriers such as cost and societal acceptance, the structured approach and comprehensive testing indicate a strong feasibility for medium- and low-voltage DC solutions in the targeted sectors.

--- phi4-normative ---
3 - Feasible

This rating reflects that, within the context of the Shift2DC project and considering the targeted sectors (data centers, buildings, industry, ports), the proposed DC solutions present a reasonable level of feasibility. The comprehensive approach to developing technologies, tools, and demonstrators across multiple application areas suggests that the initiative has identified viable pathways for integrating medium- and low-voltage DC systems into these sectors. However, given the early stage of the project and the reliance on expert input rather than extensive end-user engagement, there are still uncertainties and challenges that need to be addressed before widespread adoption can be considered very feasible. The combination of innovative technologies (such as smart cables, DC connectors, and advanced circuit breakers) with practical demonstrations in diverse settings indicates a promising direction towards overcoming some of the barriers associated with DC system integration. Nonetheless, factors such as costs, regulatory hurdles, market acceptance, and technological readiness levels across different solutions may vary, influencing the overall feasibility assessment. The rating acknowledges these complexities while recognizing the project's potential to make significant contributions to advancing DC technology adoption in targeted sectors.

The proposed technologies are innovative and address specific challenges within data centers, buildings, industry, and ports, which suggests that they have significant potential for implementation.
