LVAC-LVDC interlink converter  
Rating: 3  
Justification: It is crucial for integrating existing AC infrastructure with new DC systems, facilitating a smoother transition.  

The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays an essential role in bridging the gap between current low-voltage alternating current (LVAC) infrastructures and emerging low-voltage direct current (LVDC) solutions. By enabling seamless power conversion between these two systems, it supports the integration of DC technologies into existing buildings and industrial settings without requiring a complete overhaul of the electrical infrastructure.

This converter's ability to perform droop control reduces the need for additional downstream devices in DC architectures, enhancing system efficiency and simplifying design complexities. As such, its deployment can significantly accelerate the adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC systems across various sectors, including residential buildings and industry use cases highlighted in the Shift2DC project.

Moreover, this technology aligns with current trends towards more resilient and flexible energy systems that leverage both renewable sources and efficient power distribution networks. By facilitating a more straightforward integration process, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter addresses one of the key barriers to widespread DC adoption—namely, compatibility issues with existing AC-based infrastructure.

In summary, while it is not the sole solution required for full-scale implementation of DC systems, its role in enabling integration and minimizing transition costs makes it an important component within the broader framework of advancing DC technology adoption.
