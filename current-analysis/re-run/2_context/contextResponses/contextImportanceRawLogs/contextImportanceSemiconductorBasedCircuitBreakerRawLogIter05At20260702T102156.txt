This ensures not only the physical safety of the infrastructure but also supports broader acceptance by demonstrating a commitment to high standards of system integrity and reliability.

Given these factors, the semiconductor-based circuit breaker emerges as a critical enabler for advancing DC technology adoption, making it very important within the context of the Shift2DC initiative's goals and challenges.
