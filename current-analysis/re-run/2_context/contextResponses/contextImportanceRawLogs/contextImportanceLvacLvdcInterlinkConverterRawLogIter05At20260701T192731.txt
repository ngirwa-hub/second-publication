By enabling bidirectional conversion with built-in droop control, this technology significantly reduces complexity and cost by minimizing the number of converters needed downstream. This capability is especially important as it allows for smoother integration of renewable energy sources, which are often DC in nature, into existing infrastructure without extensive modifications. Moreover, its design aligns well with the goals of the Shift2DC initiative to promote medium- and low-voltage DC solutions across diverse sectors such as data centers, buildings, industry, and ports.

In practical applications like edge data centers and residential buildings, the ability to efficiently convert AC to DC (and vice versa) is essential for optimizing energy use and enhancing system reliability. For instance, in a micro data center setup with solar panels and V2G systems, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter can manage power flow between these components seamlessly, ensuring stability and efficiency. Similarly, in residential buildings integrating DPV systems, this technology supports the direct use of generated DC power, reducing conversion losses and improving overall energy efficiency.

The importance of this solution is further underscored by its alignment with industry standards such as Current-OS and ODCA, which are critical for interoperability and widespread adoption. By facilitating easier integration into existing electrical infrastructures, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a pivotal role in overcoming one of the major barriers to DC adoption—compatibility with legacy AC systems. This makes it not only important but essential for advancing the Shift2DC initiative's objectives.
