The static protection system's role in rapidly identifying and isolating faults within DC grids makes it a vital component for maintaining grid stability and preventing potential hazards. Given the absence of natural current zero-crossing in DC systems, traditional protection mechanisms used in AC systems are ineffective, thus necessitating specialized solutions like ultra-fast protection devices. These capabilities not only enhance operational safety but also contribute to the overall efficiency and reliability of DC microgrid applications, making it a very important solution within the Shift2DC project framework.
