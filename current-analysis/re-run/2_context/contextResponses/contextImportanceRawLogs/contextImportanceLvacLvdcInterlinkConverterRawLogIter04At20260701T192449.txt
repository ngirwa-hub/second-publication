The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a crucial role in the transition to low-voltage DC (LVDC) grids by enabling efficient conversion between AC and DC power. This capability is vital as it allows for the utilization of existing AC infrastructure while integrating new DC technologies, thus promoting widespread adoption without necessitating complete overhauls of current systems. By reducing the need for additional downstream devices through built-in droop control, this converter simplifies system design and operation, making it a key enabler in various application areas such as data centers, buildings, industry, and ports.

In the context of the Shift2DC initiative, where multiple demonstrators are planned across diverse sectors, the interlink converter's ability to bridge different power systems supports the project's goal of establishing comprehensive guidelines and roadmaps for DC adoption. Its importance is further underscored by its alignment with the need for interoperability between AC and DC technologies, addressing one of the significant technical challenges in transitioning to DC grids.

Moreover, the converter's design considerations, such as real-time monitoring and control capabilities, align well with industry standards like Current/OS and ODCA, ensuring that it meets regulatory requirements and facilitates smooth integration into existing power systems. This alignment not only enhances its practical applicability but also supports the broader objective of reducing barriers to DC technology adoption.

Overall, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter's role in facilitating the transition to a more flexible and efficient energy grid makes it an indispensable component within the Shift2DC project, warranting a very important rating.
