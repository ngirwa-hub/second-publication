This capability is essential for understanding how these technologies can be integrated into existing infrastructures and for forecasting their performance under various conditions. It allows for the exploration of different scenarios, helping to identify potential challenges and opportunities early in the planning process. This proactive approach can reduce risks associated with implementation delays and costs, making it a critical component for advancing the widespread adoption of DC technologies.

Overall, by facilitating detailed design and analysis, the network design tool significantly contributes to the effective realization of DC microgrids and other related systems, underscoring its very important role in the initiative.
