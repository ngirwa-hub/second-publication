The DC measurement device is rated very important because it provides critical data necessary for the effective operation of DC grids. By offering accurate, reproducible measurements without requiring on-site calibration, this device significantly enhances system reliability and simplifies integration processes. It aggregates multiple sensors into a single robust system, which streamlines monitoring efforts across various applications such as ports, buildings, and industrial setups highlighted in the Shift2DC project. In environments where DC systems are being piloted or scaled up, having precise measurement tools is essential for troubleshooting, maintaining efficiency, and ensuring compliance with technical standards. Furthermore, the ability to retrofit these devices easily into existing infrastructures underscores their versatility and importance in facilitating a smoother transition from AC to DC solutions. This adaptability makes them particularly valuable as they support both current operational needs and future expansions within the DC ecosystem.
