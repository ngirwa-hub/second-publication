---  

The static protection system is essential because it ensures the rapid identification and disconnection of faults within microgrids, which is crucial given the absence of natural current zero-crossing points in DC systems that complicates traditional AC protection methods. This capability enhances both safety and operational stability, making it a vital component for the successful integration of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies across various applications like data centers, buildings, industry sites, and ports. Its importance is heightened by its role in addressing one of the fundamental challenges of DC systems—ensuring reliable protection without compromising efficiency or performance.
