This makes it a critical component in enabling medium- and low-voltage DC technologies to be adopted more widely across various application areas like data centers, buildings, industry, and ports as outlined in the Shift2DC project.

Overall, its ability to simplify the transition from AC to DC systems while maintaining grid stability is pivotal for advancing renewable energy integration and supporting sustainable development goals.
