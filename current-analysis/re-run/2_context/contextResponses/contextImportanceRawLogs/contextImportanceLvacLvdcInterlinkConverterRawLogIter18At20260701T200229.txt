The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is pivotal as it facilitates the transition from traditional alternating current (AC) systems to direct current (DC) setups, particularly in environments where both types of electricity supply coexist. Given its role in reducing the number of downstream devices required and incorporating droop control, this solution directly addresses technical challenges associated with integrating DC into established AC infrastructures. This capability is especially important for medium- and low-voltage applications within sectors such as data centers, buildings, industry, and ports, where efficient energy use and integration are essential.

The converter's functionality supports the broader goals of increasing renewable energy adoption and optimizing grid performance by enabling smoother transitions and more effective power management between AC and DC systems. By reducing complexity and improving interoperability, it enhances system reliability and efficiency, which is crucial for scaling up DC solutions in diverse applications as outlined in the Shift2DC initiative.

Overall, its importance stems from its ability to enable seamless integration of renewable energy sources into existing electrical infrastructures, supporting the transition towards more sustainable energy systems. This makes it a critical component for advancing medium- and low-voltage DC technologies across various sectors.
