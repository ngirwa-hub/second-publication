DC Measurement Device  
Rating: 3  
Justification: Accurate measurement is crucial for monitoring and optimizing DC grid performance.  

---

The DC measurement device plays an essential role in the Shift2DC project by enabling precise data collection, which supports effective management and optimization of DC systems. It allows for real-time insights into voltage, current, and temperature metrics without requiring on-site calibration, making it a vital tool for ensuring efficient system operation and troubleshooting. The ability to aggregate multiple sensors into a single robust measurement system further enhances its importance by simplifying installation and maintenance processes across various demonstration sites, including buildings, industry, and ports. By facilitating accurate measurements, this device contributes significantly to the reliable implementation of DC technologies, aligning with the project's goals of promoting sustainable energy systems and overcoming existing technical challenges in medium- and low-voltage DC applications.
