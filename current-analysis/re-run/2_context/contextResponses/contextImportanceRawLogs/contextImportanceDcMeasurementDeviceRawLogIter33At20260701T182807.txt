---

The DC measurement device plays a pivotal role in the successful implementation and operation of medium- and low-voltage DC grids. By enabling accurate and reproducible measurements without on-site calibration, it ensures that system performance can be reliably assessed and optimized. This capability is especially important given the nascent stage of DC technology adoption; reliable data is essential for building confidence among stakeholders and addressing concerns about new technologies.

Furthermore, integrating multiple sensors into a single robust measurement system simplifies installation and maintenance processes, reducing operational complexities. This streamlining is particularly valuable in diverse application areas like data centers, buildings, industrial settings, and ports where the conditions can vary significantly. The ease of retrofit installation enhances its applicability across existing infrastructures without necessitating extensive modifications.

In summary, the DC measurement device's ability to provide precise, real-time insights into system performance makes it a cornerstone for both operational efficiency and strategic planning in the deployment of DC solutions. As such, its importance is rated as "Very important" within the context of the Shift2DC initiative's objectives to foster widespread adoption of DC technologies.
