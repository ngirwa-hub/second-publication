The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a pivotal role in the transition to low-voltage DC grids by enabling efficient power conversion between existing AC infrastructure and emerging DC technologies. This capability is essential for integrating renewable energy sources, which often generate DC power, into traditional AC-based electrical systems. By incorporating built-in droop control, the converter minimizes the complexity and cost of additional devices required downstream in the DC architecture, enhancing overall system efficiency and reliability.

In the context of the Shift2DC project, where multiple demonstrators across data centers, buildings, industry, and ports are being developed, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is particularly important. For instance, in residential buildings with distributed photovoltaic systems or industrial setups with large facilities connected to DC grids, the ability to seamlessly convert between AC and DC can significantly streamline operations and improve energy efficiency.

Moreover, the converter's role extends beyond mere technical functionality; it addresses key barriers to DC adoption by simplifying system design and reducing costs associated with integrating new technologies. This aligns with the project's goals of establishing guidelines and roadmaps for widespread DC adoption, making the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter a vital component in achieving these objectives.
