Static protection system

Justification: Rapid fault isolation is crucial for safety and reliability in DC microgrids.  

---

The static protection system stands out as a very important component in the context of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. As DC systems do not have natural current zero-crossing points like AC systems, they require specialized solutions to detect and isolate faults quickly to prevent damage and maintain safety. The advanced fault detection capabilities and ultra-fast protection devices described are essential for ensuring the stability and resilience of DC microgrids, particularly in applications involving renewable energy integration where power fluctuations can be more frequent. This makes it a critical element in supporting the widespread adoption and reliability of DC solutions.
