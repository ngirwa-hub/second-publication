Justification: It streamlines the design and integration of DC systems, crucial for widespread adoption.

This tool is particularly important because it addresses both technical and social challenges by making complex DC system designs more accessible and understandable, which can lead to greater acceptance and trust among stakeholders. Overall, its comprehensive features make it a cornerstone for the successful deployment of DC solutions within the Shift2DC initiative.
