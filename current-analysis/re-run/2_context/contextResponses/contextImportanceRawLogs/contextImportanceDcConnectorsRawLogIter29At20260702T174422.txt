DC connectors play a pivotal role in the adoption and functionality of direct current (DC) technologies, as they provide the necessary interface between different components within a system. The development of both passive and smart connectors addresses key aspects such as physical robustness and advanced electronic capabilities like handling higher voltages and currents. These features are essential for ensuring the safe, efficient, and reliable operation of DC systems in diverse environments, including buildings and ports. By facilitating easier integration and enhancing interoperability among different DC components, these connectors significantly contribute to overcoming barriers related to adoption and implementation of medium- and low-voltage DC solutions. Furthermore, as DC technologies continue to evolve, the ability to seamlessly connect various devices and systems will be critical in maximizing their potential benefits, making the development of robust and versatile DC connectors a very important aspect of advancing DC technology deployment.
