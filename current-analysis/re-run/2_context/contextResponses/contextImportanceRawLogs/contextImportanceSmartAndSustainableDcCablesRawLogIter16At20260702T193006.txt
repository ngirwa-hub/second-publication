Rating: 4  
Justification: They are fundamental for ensuring efficient, long-lasting connections across various DC applications.  

---

The rationale behind this high importance rating is that smart and sustainable DC cables serve as the backbone of any DC system by providing reliable power transmission with minimal environmental impact. Their ergonomic design mimics standard AC handling, which facilitates their adoption in diverse settings such as data centers, buildings, industries, and ports. Given their role across all demonstration sites in the Shift2DC project, these cables are critical for achieving seamless integration and operation of DC technologies, thus supporting widespread adoption.
