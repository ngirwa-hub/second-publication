Moreover, the converter's role in reducing the number of necessary downstream devices not only streamlines system architecture but also lowers installation and maintenance costs. This cost-effectiveness is particularly beneficial for early adopters of DC technology who may be cautious about initial investments.

In summary, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is very important due to its ability to integrate AC and DC systems efficiently, support renewable energy integration, reduce system complexity, and lower costs, thereby facilitating broader adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC solutions.
