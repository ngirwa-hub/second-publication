The Network design tool is critical because it supports the visualization and testing of different DC ecosystems before physical implementation, reducing risks and potential costs associated with trial-and-error approaches in real-world applications. By integrating static models of key devices, it aids stakeholders in making informed decisions on infrastructure investments and operational strategies.

This tool's importance is further underscored by its role in facilitating a smoother transition to DC systems by providing insights into system behavior under various conditions and configurations. It helps identify potential bottlenecks or inefficiencies, ensuring that the designed network meets performance expectations while adhering to standards like Current/OS and ODCA.

Moreover, as the Shift2DC project aims to establish guidelines and roadmaps for DC adoption, having a reliable design tool is essential for creating replicable and scalable models. This contributes significantly to the broader goal of mainstreaming medium- and low-voltage DC solutions across different sectors, from data centers to ports, by enabling stakeholders to predict outcomes accurately and plan accordingly.
