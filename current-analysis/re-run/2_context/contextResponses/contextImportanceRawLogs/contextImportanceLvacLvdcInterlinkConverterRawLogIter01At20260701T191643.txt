The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a pivotal role in the transition from traditional AC to modern DC power systems. By bridging these two types of electrical systems, it enables seamless integration and interoperability, which are essential for widespread acceptance and implementation of DC solutions. This capability is particularly important given the existing prevalence of AC infrastructure worldwide.

The converter's built-in droop control further enhances its importance by reducing the need for additional downstream devices in a DC architecture. This not only simplifies system design but also potentially lowers costs and increases efficiency, making it an attractive solution for both new installations and retrofitting existing systems.

Moreover, as renewable energy sources like solar panels (which inherently produce DC power) become more common, having efficient converters that can manage the transition between AC and DC is vital. This supports the integration of renewable energy into the grid, aligning with global sustainability goals and reducing reliance on fossil fuels.

In summary, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is a cornerstone technology for advancing medium- and low-voltage DC solutions, making it very important in the context of the Shift2DC initiative.
