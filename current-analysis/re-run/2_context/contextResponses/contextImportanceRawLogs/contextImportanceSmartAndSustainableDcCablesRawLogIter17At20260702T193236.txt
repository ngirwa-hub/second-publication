In addition to their role in easing the transition to DC power systems, the environmental sustainability aspect of these cables aligns with broader global objectives towards reducing carbon footprints and promoting renewable energy sources. As the project aims to integrate renewables more effectively into the grid, ensuring that all components, including transmission mediums like cables, adhere to sustainable practices is paramount.
