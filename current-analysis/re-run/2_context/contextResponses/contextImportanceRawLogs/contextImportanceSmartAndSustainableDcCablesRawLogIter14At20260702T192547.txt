The smart and sustainable DC cables stand out as a very important component within the Shift2DC initiative. Their design prioritizes durability, environmental friendliness, and ergonomic use, which are essential attributes to facilitate widespread adoption across various demonstration sites like data centers, buildings, industry facilities, and ports. By ensuring comparable or improved longevity relative to AC cables while maintaining ease of handling similar to standard AC cables, these DC solutions address a significant barrier in the transition from AC to DC systems. Their critical role is further underscored by their universal applicability across all demonstration sites, which helps streamline integration processes and reduce resistance due to unfamiliarity with new technologies.

Furthermore, as the Shift2DC project aims to establish guidelines and roadmaps for adopting medium- and low-voltage DC solutions, having robust, sustainable cable infrastructure supports both the technological and practical aspects of this transition. The cables' design aligns with broader sustainability goals and contributes to minimizing the environmental impact associated with large-scale deployments of new power technologies.

Overall, by addressing key challenges related to material durability, user adoption ease, and environmental considerations, smart and sustainable DC cables play a pivotal role in advancing the objectives of the Shift2DC initiative.
