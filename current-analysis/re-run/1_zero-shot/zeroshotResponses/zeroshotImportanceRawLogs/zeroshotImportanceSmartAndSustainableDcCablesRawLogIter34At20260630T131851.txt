---

**Detailed Justification:** Smart and sustainable DC cables play a pivotal role in the Shift2DC project by enabling more efficient energy transmission with reduced losses compared to traditional AC systems. Their ability to integrate seamlessly into modern infrastructures makes them vital for applications such as high-voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission, which is essential for connecting remote renewable energy sources like offshore wind farms to urban centers. Additionally, these cables support the development of low-voltage DC grids within buildings and data centers, optimizing power delivery for sensitive electronic equipment and reducing overall energy consumption. Their smart features allow for real-time monitoring and adaptive control, further enhancing reliability and operational efficiency in complex environments such as ports and industrial settings. This adaptability is critical given the growing demand for sustainable and resilient infrastructure amidst increasing global energy needs and environmental concerns.
