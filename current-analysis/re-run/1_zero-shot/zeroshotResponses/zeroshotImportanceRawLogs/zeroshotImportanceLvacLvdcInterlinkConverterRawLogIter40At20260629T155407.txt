The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a crucial role in the Shift2DC project by enabling direct current (DC) technologies to integrate smoothly with legacy alternating current (AC) systems. This is particularly important in ports, industrial settings, data centers, and buildings where both AC and DC infrastructures coexist. By allowing for efficient conversion between AC and DC power, these converters help optimize energy use, reduce losses, and improve overall system efficiency.

One of the key benefits of the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is its ability to minimize conversion losses that typically occur when converting between AC and DC multiple times. This is especially beneficial in environments where renewable energy sources (such as solar panels or battery storage systems) are prevalent, as these often generate DC power. By reducing the need for double conversions (DC-AC-DC), energy efficiency is improved, leading to cost savings and reduced carbon footprints.

Moreover, LVAC-LVDC interlink converters support the flexibility and scalability of hybrid power systems. They enable facilities to gradually transition from AC-dominated infrastructures to more DC-centric ones without requiring a complete overhaul of existing systems. This adaptability is vital for modernizing energy infrastructure incrementally while maintaining operational continuity.

In addition, these converters contribute to improved reliability and stability in power distribution networks. By facilitating efficient power flow between AC and DC grids, they help balance loads and mitigate issues such as voltage sags or surges that can occur during peak demand periods. This is particularly relevant in data centers and industrial settings where consistent and reliable power supply is critical for operations.

Overall, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is an important component in advancing the adoption of DC technologies within mixed AC/DC environments, supporting both current operational needs and future energy transition goals.
