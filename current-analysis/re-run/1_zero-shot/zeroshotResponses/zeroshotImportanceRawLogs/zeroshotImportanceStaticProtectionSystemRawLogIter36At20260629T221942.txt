Static Protection System  
Rating: 3  
Justification: Static protection systems are crucial for preventing electrical discharge, ensuring safety and equipment integrity in DC applications.  

---

Static protection systems play a critical role in protecting sensitive electronic components from static electricity discharges, which can be particularly damaging in environments like ports, data centers, and industrial settings where the Shift2DC project is active. These systems help maintain operational reliability by preventing unexpected failures or damages due to electrostatic discharge (ESD). Their importance is underscored in DC applications where power levels are often higher than in AC systems, making the potential impact of static discharges more significant. Therefore, integrating a robust static protection system is essential for ensuring safety and continuity in these environments.
