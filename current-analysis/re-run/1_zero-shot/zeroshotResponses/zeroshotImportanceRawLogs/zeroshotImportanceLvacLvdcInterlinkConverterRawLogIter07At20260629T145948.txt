It allows seamless conversion between low-voltage AC (LVAC) and low-voltage DC (LVDC), enabling legacy AC infrastructure to support modern DC loads without extensive modifications. This adaptability reduces the need for costly overhauls, making it a pivotal solution in the gradual shift towards DC power systems.

The converter's ability to bridge existing AC networks with new DC applications supports the efficient use of renewable energy sources, which often generate DC power. By minimizing conversion losses and enhancing system flexibility, LVAC-LVDC interlink converters play an essential role in optimizing energy efficiency and reducing carbon footprints across various industries.
