---

The semiconductor-based circuit breaker is pivotal in DC systems due to its ability to rapidly interrupt fault currents without the arcing issues present in traditional mechanical breakers. This rapid response capability enhances system reliability and safety, especially in environments like ports, industry, data centers, and buildings where power demands are substantial and interruptions can have significant impacts. The integration of such technology supports the broader adoption of DC solutions by addressing critical challenges associated with high-voltage DC transmission and distribution systems. Additionally, semiconductor circuit breakers contribute to improved energy efficiency and reduced maintenance costs, aligning well with the goals of the Shift2DC project in promoting sustainable and resilient power infrastructure.
