--- 

Smart and sustainable DC cables are crucial for optimizing energy transmission by minimizing losses that typically occur during conversion between AC and DC systems. This is particularly important in ports, industrial settings, data centers, and buildings, where high power demands necessitate efficient energy delivery to maintain sustainability goals. By reducing these losses, smart DC cables not only improve operational efficiency but also contribute to lower carbon footprints, aligning with global efforts toward greener infrastructure. Additionally, their adaptability to incorporate advanced monitoring technologies supports more responsive and resilient energy management systems, further underscoring their importance in the transition to sustainable energy practices.
