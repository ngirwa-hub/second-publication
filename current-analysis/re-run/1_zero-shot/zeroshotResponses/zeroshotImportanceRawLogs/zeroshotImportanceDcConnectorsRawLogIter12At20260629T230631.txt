---  

The Shift2DC project emphasizes the transition to direct current (DC) technologies in several sectors such as ports, industrial facilities, data centers, and buildings. This initiative highlights the growing importance of DC solutions, which aim to improve efficiency, reduce energy losses, and enhance the integration of renewable energy sources.

In this context, evaluating DC connectors is particularly relevant because they play a fundamental role in facilitating the interoperability of different devices and systems operating on DC power. By providing standardized interfaces for connecting equipment, DC connectors help ensure that diverse components can work together seamlessly, which is essential for optimizing the performance and reliability of DC microgrids and other DC-based systems.

Moreover, the adoption of DC technology often involves transitioning from existing AC infrastructure to new setups that can directly utilize renewable energy sources like solar panels. In these scenarios, efficient and reliable connectors are necessary to manage power distribution safely and effectively. Therefore, given their pivotal role in enabling a smooth transition to DC technologies across various sectors, DC connectors receive a high importance rating within the Shift2DC project framework.
