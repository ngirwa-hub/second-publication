By incorporating static protection systems, stakeholders can enhance public trust in these technologies, thereby accelerating their adoption. The assurance of safety provided by such systems can be a decisive factor for industries considering the transition to DC power solutions.

In summary, the static protection system's role in preventing electrical hazards makes it very important within the context of promoting DC technologies across various sectors. Its implementation supports ethical standards, regulatory compliance, and social acceptance while contributing significantly to operational reliability and sustainability.
