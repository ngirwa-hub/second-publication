The network design tool is crucial because it enables the efficient planning, simulation, and management of DC power systems, which are integral to the Shift2DC project's goals of promoting sustainable energy use in critical infrastructures such as ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. This tool facilitates the seamless integration of renewable energy sources by allowing for precise modeling of network configurations, optimizing load balancing, and ensuring system reliability.

Moreover, it helps address engineering challenges associated with high-voltage DC transmission and low-voltage DC grids, including insulation requirements and power conversion efficiency. By providing insights into potential interoperability issues and regulatory barriers, the tool supports informed decision-making that aligns with industry standards and emerging innovations in DC technologies.

Additionally, by reducing infrastructure costs through efficient design and deployment strategies, the network design tool plays a pivotal role in accelerating the adoption of DC solutions, ultimately contributing to a more sustainable and resilient energy landscape.
