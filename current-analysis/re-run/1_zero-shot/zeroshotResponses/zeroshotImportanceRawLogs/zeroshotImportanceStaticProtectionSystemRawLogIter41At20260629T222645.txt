---  

A Static Protection System is crucial in environments where Direct Current (DC) technologies are implemented, such as ports, industrial facilities, data centers, and buildings. These systems protect against voltage spikes and transients that can damage or disrupt the operation of electronic devices and power equipment. As DC technology becomes more prevalent, especially given its efficiency benefits over traditional AC systems in certain applications, ensuring robust protection measures is critical to maintaining operational stability and safety. This makes a Static Protection System very important within the context of promoting DC solutions in these sectors.
