---

Smart and sustainable DC cables play a pivotal role in modernizing power infrastructure by improving the efficiency of electricity distribution and minimizing losses. In the context of the Shift2DC project's focus on ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, these cables are invaluable due to their ability to handle high-voltage DC transmission over long distances with reduced energy loss compared to traditional AC systems.

The integration of smart technologies within these cables allows for real-time monitoring and management of power flow, which is crucial in dynamic environments like industrial settings and data centers where load demands can fluctuate significantly. This capability ensures optimal performance and reliability, reducing downtime and maintenance costs.

Moreover, sustainable DC cables contribute to environmental goals by facilitating the integration of renewable energy sources such as solar and wind into the grid. By enabling more efficient transmission of electricity generated from these sources, they help reduce reliance on fossil fuels and lower carbon emissions, aligning with global sustainability initiatives.

In summary, smart and sustainable DC cables are not only important but essential for advancing the efficiency, reliability, and environmental impact of modern power systems, particularly in sectors targeted by the Shift2DC project.
