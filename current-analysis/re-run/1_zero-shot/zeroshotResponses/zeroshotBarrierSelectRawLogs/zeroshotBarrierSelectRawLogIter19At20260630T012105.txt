Based on the provided context, here are five barriers I would select for the Shift2DC project:

1. Power losses, quality, and safety issues - The transition to DC technologies may encounter challenges related to power loss, quality, and safety concerns due to the need for conversion between AC and DC systems.
2. Uncertain utility interaction (net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards) - The integration of DC technologies with existing energy infrastructure might face complications due to unclear rules and agreements regarding net metering, utility ownership, and standardization.
3. Lack of pilot projects - A limited number of pilot projects may hinder the development and demonstration of successful DC solutions, making it difficult for stakeholders to assess their viability and potential benefits.
4. Incompatibility of DC systems components - The compatibility issues among various DC system components could pose challenges in terms of interoperability and integration within existing energy infrastructure.
5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems - Insufficient expertise in DC technologies might lead to difficulties in designing, installing, and maintaining DC systems, potentially slowing down the transition to a sustainable energy system based on DC technologies.
