1. power losses, quality and safety issues
2. reduced reliability in DC devices
3. uncertain utility interaction (net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards)
4. lack of pilot projects
5. uncertain regulatory roadmap

These five barriers were selected due to their direct impact on the adoption and implementation of DC technologies in various sectors. Power losses, quality, and safety issues are a significant concern as they can affect the efficiency and reliability of DC power systems. Reduced reliability in DC devices can lead to increased maintenance costs and potential system failures. Uncertain utility interaction can create barriers for net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards, which are crucial for the integration of renewable energy sources into the grid. Lack of pilot projects hinders the demonstration and validation of the benefits of DC technologies in real-world applications. Lastly, an uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and project developers, potentially slowing down the adoption of DC technologies.
