5. uncertain regulatory roadmap

These barriers are directly related to the integration and adoption of direct current (DC) technologies in various sectors. Power losses, quality, and safety issues are significant concerns when transitioning to DC systems. Reduced reliability in DC devices can lead to operational challenges and increased costs. Uncertain utility interaction is a critical issue as it affects net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards, which are essential for the seamless integration of DC technologies. Lack of pilot projects hinders the demonstration of the practical benefits of DC solutions, making it difficult to convince stakeholders of their viability. Lastly, an uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and project developers, potentially slowing down the transition to DC technologies.
