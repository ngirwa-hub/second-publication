These barriers have been identified as significant challenges in the widespread adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. They include issues related to public acceptance, regulatory uncertainties, power loss and safety concerns, the need for more pilot projects and demonstrations, and a lack of clear use-cases where DC is preferable. Addressing these barriers will be crucial for the successful implementation and growth of DC technologies in various applications.
