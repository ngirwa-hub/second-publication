- uncertain regulatory roadmap

These barriers were selected as they directly impact the adoption and implementation of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. Incompatibility and lack of trained personnel can hinder the integration of DC systems, while power losses, safety issues, and regulatory uncertainties can increase costs and complicate the design and permit process. Reduced reliability in DC devices can also impact the overall efficiency and effectiveness of DC systems.
