These barriers have been identified as significant challenges in the widespread adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. Incompatibility of components can hinder seamless integration, while misconceptions and lack of knowledge can lead to complex and costly design and permit processes. Uncertain utility interactions and regulatory issues can create barriers to net metering and standardization, and the absence of pilot projects can limit the demonstration of DC technologies' benefits. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can further complicate the adoption of DC technologies.
