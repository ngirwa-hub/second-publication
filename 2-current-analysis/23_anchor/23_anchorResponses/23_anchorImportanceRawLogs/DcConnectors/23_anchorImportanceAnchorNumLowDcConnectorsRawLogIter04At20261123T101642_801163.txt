DC connectors  
Rating: 4  
Justification: They enable robust, high-voltage, and current handling crucial for efficient DC microgrids, supporting widespread adoption.

DC connectors are pivotal in facilitating the integration and reliability of DC systems, particularly in medium- and low-voltage applications. Their development into both passive and smart variants addresses the need for physical robustness and advanced functionalities like microelectronics, which are essential for handling higher voltages and currents in modern DC grids. This capability is crucial for maintaining the efficiency and reliability of DC microgrids, which are reported to improve distribution efficiency by 2–6% under efficient baselines. Therefore, the advancement of DC connectors is very important for the successful deployment and operational efficiency of DC solutions.
