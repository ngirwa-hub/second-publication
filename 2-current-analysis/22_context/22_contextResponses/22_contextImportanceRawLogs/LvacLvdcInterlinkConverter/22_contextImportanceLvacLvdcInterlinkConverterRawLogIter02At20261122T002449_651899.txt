The LVAC-LVDC interlink converter plays a pivotal role in bridging the gap between AC and DC power systems, which is essential for the successful integration of renewable energy sources and DC technologies. This converter facilitates seamless power flow between the two systems, ensuring efficient energy distribution and utilization. By incorporating built-in droop control, it minimizes the complexity and cost associated with additional downstream devices, making it a cost-effective solution for expanding DC infrastructure. Furthermore, its ability to support multiple DC ecosystem configurations enhances its adaptability to various applications, from residential buildings to industrial settings. The converter's design aligns with the goals of the Shift2DC initiative by promoting the widespread adoption of DC solutions and addressing the technical challenges of interoperability between AC and DC grids. Its importance is underscored by its contribution to reducing energy losses during conversion, improving overall system efficiency, and supporting the transition towards more sustainable energy systems. In summary, the LVAC-LVDC interlink converter is a key enabler of the shift towards DC, offering significant benefits in terms of system integration, efficiency, and scalability.
