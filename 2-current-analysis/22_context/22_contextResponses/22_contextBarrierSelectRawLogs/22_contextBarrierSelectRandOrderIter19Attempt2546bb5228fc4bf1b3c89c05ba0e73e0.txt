- lack of pilot projects

These barriers were selected based on their potential impact on the widespread adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. The first three barriers, misconception and lack of knowledge, incompatibility, and uncertain regulatory roadmap, are crucial for addressing the design, compatibility, and regulatory challenges that may arise during the implementation of DC solutions. The fourth barrier, lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems, is essential for ensuring the availability of skilled professionals to manage and maintain DC systems. The fifth barrier, lack of pilot projects, is important for demonstrating the feasibility and advantages of DC technologies to potential users and stakeholders.
