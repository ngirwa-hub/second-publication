--- mistral-normative ---
- public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects
- power losses, quality and safety issues
- uncertain regulatory roadmap
- incompatibility of DC systems components
- lack of pilot projects

These barriers have been selected as they present challenges in the widespread adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC solutions. Public perception and lack of champions can hinder the growth of DC projects, while power losses, quality, and safety issues can affect the efficiency and reliability of DC systems. Uncertain regulatory roadmaps can create uncertainty for investors and project developers, and incompatibility of DC systems components can limit the scalability and interoperability of DC solutions. Lastly, the absence of pilot projects can hinder the development and demonstration of successful DC projects, which can in turn impact the confidence of stakeholders in the technology.
