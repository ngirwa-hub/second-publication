- high costs of DC solutions
- power losses, quality and safety issues
- uncertain regulatory roadmap
- incompatibility of DC systems components

These barriers are crucial to consider when promoting the widespread adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. Lack of trained personnel and high costs can hinder the implementation and operation of DC systems, while power losses, quality, and safety issues can impact the efficiency and reliability of DC grids. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainties for investors and developers, and incompatibility of DC systems components can limit the scalability and interoperability of DC solutions. Public perception and the readiness to champion installations from DC projects are also essential factors to consider, as they can influence the acceptance and adoption of DC technologies by end-users.
