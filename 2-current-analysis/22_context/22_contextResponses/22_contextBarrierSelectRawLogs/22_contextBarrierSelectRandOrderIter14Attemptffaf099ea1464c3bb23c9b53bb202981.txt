These five barriers, as selected, highlight key challenges in the widespread adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. The first barrier, power losses, quality, and safety issues, is a major concern due to the potential for increased energy losses and potential safety hazards in DC systems. The second barrier, misconception and lack of knowledge, can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, as there may be a lack of understanding about the specific needs and benefits of DC technologies. The third barrier, reduced reliability in DC devices, is a concern due to the need for reliable and robust components in these systems. The fourth barrier, lack of pilot projects, can hinder the development and demonstration of DC technologies, making it difficult for stakeholders to assess their feasibility and potential impact. The fifth barrier, uncertain regulatory roadmap, is a significant concern as regulatory frameworks play a crucial role in shaping the market for DC technologies and influencing their adoption rates.
