These barriers highlight some of the key challenges in the widespread adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. High costs, a lack of trained personnel, and misconceptions can hinder the implementation of these technologies. Uncertain utility interactions and the absence of pilot projects can create barriers to their deployment. Addressing these challenges will be crucial for the successful integration of DC solutions in various application areas, such as data centers, buildings, industry, and ports.
