These five barriers have been selected based on their potential to significantly impact the widespread adoption of medium- and low-voltage DC technologies. High costs and a lack of clear use-cases for DC technologies are major hurdles that need to be addressed to make DC solutions more competitive with traditional AC systems. Incompatibility of components and uncertain utility interactions can also pose challenges in integrating DC systems into existing infrastructure. Lastly, regulatory uncertainties can create obstacles for the development and deployment of DC technologies.
