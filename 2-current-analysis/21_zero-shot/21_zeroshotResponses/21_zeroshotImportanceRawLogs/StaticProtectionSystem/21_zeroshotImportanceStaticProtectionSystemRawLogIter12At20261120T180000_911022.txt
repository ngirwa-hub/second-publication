---

The static protection system is crucial in environments like ports, industries, data centers, and buildings where DC technologies are being promoted by the Shift2DC project. These environments are susceptible to lightning strikes and static discharge, which can cause significant damage to DC systems if not properly managed. Static protection systems are designed to safeguard these infrastructures by safely dissipating static electricity and lightning energy, thereby preventing equipment damage and ensuring operational continuity.

In ports, where large metallic structures and equipment are prevalent, the risk of lightning strikes is high. Static protection systems are essential to protect sensitive DC equipment and communication systems that are integral to port operations. Similarly, in industrial settings, machinery and automated systems often rely on DC power, making them vulnerable to electrical surges. Static protection systems help maintain the integrity and reliability of these systems, preventing costly downtimes and equipment failures.

Data centers and buildings that utilize DC power for energy efficiency and reliability also benefit significantly from static protection systems. These systems ensure that the sensitive electronic components and servers remain operational and protected from electrical disturbances. Given the increasing adoption of DC technologies in these areas, the importance of static protection systems cannot be overstated, as they play a vital role in maintaining the safety and efficiency of DC power infrastructures.
