--- mistral-generalist ---
- incompatibility of DC systems components
- misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
- uncertain regulatory roadmap
- high costs of DC solutions
- lack of pilot projects

These five barriers are crucial in the transition to DC technologies. Incompatibility of components can hinder the seamless integration of DC systems, while misconceptions and lack of knowledge can lead to expensive and lengthy design and permit processes. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and developers. High costs of DC solutions can deter adoption, and the lack of pilot projects can hinder the demonstration of the benefits of DC technologies.
