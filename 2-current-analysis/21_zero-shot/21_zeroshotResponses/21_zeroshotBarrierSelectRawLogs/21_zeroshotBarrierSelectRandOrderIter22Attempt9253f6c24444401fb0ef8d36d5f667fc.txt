--- mistral-subject-matter ---
- power losses, quality and safety issues
- incompatibility of DC systems components
- lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
- high costs of DC solutions
- uncertain regulatory roadmap

These barriers are significant challenges that need to be addressed in order to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using direct current (DC) technologies. Power losses, quality, and safety issues are crucial concerns as they impact the efficiency and reliability of DC power systems. Incompatibility of DC systems components can hinder the integration of various components in a system, leading to increased costs and potential system failures. A lack of trained personnel in DC systems can slow down the adoption and implementation of DC technologies, as skilled workers are essential for the design, installation, and maintenance of these systems. High costs of DC solutions can be a barrier to widespread adoption, particularly in regions with limited financial resources. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty and slow down the adoption of DC technologies, as regulations play a critical role in shaping the market for these technologies.

The Shift2DC project aims to address these challenges and promote the use of DC technologies in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. Through the development and demonstration of DC solutions, the project aims to demonstrate the advantages of DC technologies and promote their widespread adoption. By addressing these barriers, the project aims to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using direct current (DC) technologies.
