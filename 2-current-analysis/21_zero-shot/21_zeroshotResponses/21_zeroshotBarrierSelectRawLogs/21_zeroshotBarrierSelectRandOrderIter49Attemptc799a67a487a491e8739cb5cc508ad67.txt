These barriers, as per the provided list, present challenges in the transition to a DC-based sustainable energy system. Uncertain regulatory roadmap refers to the lack of clear guidelines and policies from regulatory bodies, which can hinder the adoption of DC technologies. Reduced reliability in DC devices implies that the current DC devices may not be as reliable as their AC counterparts, which can impact the overall system performance. High costs of DC solutions are a significant barrier due to the initial investment required for implementing DC technologies. Lack of pilot projects is a challenge as they are essential for demonstrating the feasibility and benefits of DC solutions. Lastly, incompatibility of DC systems components indicates that the existing components may not be compatible with each other, which can create technical challenges during implementation.
