- uncertain regulatory roadmap

These five barriers are significant in the transition to a sustainable energy system promoted by the Shift2DC project. Uncertainty in utility interaction, lack of use-cases, public perception, lack of trained personnel, and unclear regulatory roadmap are key challenges that need to be addressed to accelerate the adoption of DC technologies in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. High costs and incompatibility of DC systems components are also important barriers that need to be overcome to make DC solutions more attractive and viable.
