--- mistral-generalist ---
- incompatibility of DC systems components
- uncertain regulatory roadmap
- high costs of DC solutions
- power losses, quality and safety issues
- lack of pilot projects

These five barriers were selected as they represent significant challenges in the transition to a sustainable energy system using direct current (DC) technologies, as outlined in the Shift2DC project. Incompatibility of DC systems components can hinder the seamless integration of various technologies, an uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and developers, high costs of DC solutions can make them less competitive compared to traditional technologies, power losses, quality, and safety issues can impact the efficiency and reliability of DC systems, and the lack of pilot projects can make it difficult to demonstrate the benefits and feasibility of DC technologies. Addressing these barriers is crucial for the successful implementation and widespread adoption of DC technologies in the targeted sectors (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings) and for accelerating the transition to a sustainable energy system.
