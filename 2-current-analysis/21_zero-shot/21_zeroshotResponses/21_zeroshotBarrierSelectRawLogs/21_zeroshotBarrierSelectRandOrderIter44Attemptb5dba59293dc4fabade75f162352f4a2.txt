--- mistral-generalist ---
- Lack of use-cases in which DC is advantageous
- Uncertain regulatory roadmap
- Incompatibility of DC systems components
- High costs of DC solutions
- Lack of pilot projects

These barriers pose significant challenges to the widespread adoption of DC technologies. Lack of use-cases and pilot projects hinders the demonstration of DC's benefits, making it difficult for stakeholders to invest in this technology. Uncertain regulatory roadmaps and high costs make it challenging for DC solutions to compete with established, traditional energy systems. Incompatibility among DC systems components further complicates the transition to DC technologies, as it requires standardization and collaboration among manufacturers. Public perception and the lack of trained personnel also contribute to the slow adoption of DC technologies.
