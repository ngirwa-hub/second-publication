--- mistral-subject-matter ---
- lack of use-cases in which DC is advantageous
- reduced reliability in DC devices
- uncertain regulatory roadmap
- lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
- high costs of DC solutions

These barriers are significant in the transition to a DC-based energy system. The lack of demonstrated use-cases where DC is advantageous can hinder the adoption of these technologies. Reduced reliability in DC devices can lead to system failures and increased maintenance costs. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and project developers. A lack of trained personnel in DC systems can lead to inefficiencies and increased costs in project development and implementation. Lastly, high costs of DC solutions can make these technologies less attractive to potential adopters compared to traditional AC systems. Addressing these barriers is crucial for the successful implementation of the Shift2DC project.
