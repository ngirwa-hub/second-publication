Four demonstrators are planned across data centers, buildings, industry, and ports, described as follows.

Data center

A data center demonstrator target to explore edge data centers and their integration with renewables and heat reuse. It also examines powering office loads via DC, not only computing equipment. The setup includes vehicle-to-grid (V2G) and solar panels and will operate as a real micro data center.

Building

A residential building demonstrator will test how existing DC technologies can be integrated into a DC grid. It includes a live setup and focuses on DC-related technologies, including distributed photovoltaic (DPV) systems.

Industry

Two industrial use cases are planned. One demonstrator focuses on integrating technologies and connecting large facilities. Another examines the integration of renewables and chargers at around 100 kW scale. A separate industry living-lab setup connects two buildings using DC technology.

Port

A port demonstrator is planned to assess the feasibility of DC for onshore power supply in port environments. It is small in scale and includes a digital twin to explore how DC solutions could be scaled in the port. In addition, it tests multiple technologies and considers end-user perspectives, including passers-by who may not realize the system is DC. The installation includes approximately 10 kW of PV and 11 kW of V2G chargers.
