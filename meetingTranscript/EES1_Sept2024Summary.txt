SHIFT2DC T1.6: Pilot of the Expert Elicitation Protocol
Mon, Sep 23, 2024

Summary:

The meeting centered around the Shift to DC project, which aims to create guidelines and roadmaps for the adoption of medium and low-voltage direct current (DC) solutions. Lucas Pereira initiated the session by introducing the project and facilitating a round of introductions among participants, who are experts in energy and DC systems. This initial segment set the groundwork for subsequent discussions, highlighting the expertise of the attendees and their roles in advancing the project's objectives.

Pereira elaborated on the project's organization, emphasizing the development of software and hardware tools for simulation and demonstration across various industrial sectors. Key focus areas include DC grids, IT solutions, prototypes, and feasibility studies. The conversation then shifted to technical aspects, where Stammberger and Pereira compared static and semiconductor-based protection systems, while Lina Ruiz provided insights into the specifications for DC cables, particularly in building applications. The development of semiconductor-based circuit breakers and DC-DC converters was also discussed, alongside the creation of a software tool for designing and testing protection systems.

The meeting utilized Slido to facilitate real-time questions and answers, allowing participants to express their views on the feasibility and significance of DC technologies across different sectors. Pereira led discussions on sustainable materials and design tools, with contributions from Stammberger and Bartonek. The team identified barriers to adoption, notably the shortage of trained personnel and high costs, which were highlighted as significant challenges to the project's success. Additionally, Pereira and Ceballos Recio addressed the difficulty in obtaining meaningful comparisons of technology benefits without detailed studies, emphasizing the need for clear incentives for adopting DC solutions.

Further discussions delved into the cost implications of various components necessary for large-scale deployment of DC systems. Participants, including Carlos Castro, Katharina Hetzenecker, and Lina Ruiz, shared insights on how cost affects market traction, engineering challenges, and the lifespan of DC cables. The importance of standards in reducing costs and the potential role of open-source solutions were also examined. The meeting concluded with a risk assessment of project components, including DC cables and semiconductor circuit breakers, and a discussion on the necessity for smart, sustainable solutions in light of regulatory impacts on cable usage.


Chapters & Topics:

Introduction and Project Overview
Lucas Pereira provides an overview of the Shift to DC project, outlining its objective to establish guidelines and roadmaps for the widespread adoption of DC solutions. He then conducts a round of introductions with the participants, who are experts in energy and DC systems aligned with the project, allowing each to briefly introduce themselves and their roles in the project.

Overview of Project Organization and Development of Tools
Lucas Pereira provides an overview of the project's organization, emphasizing the development of software and hardware tools for simulation and demonstration in different industrial domains. He highlights the focus on DC grids, IT solutions, prototypes with high DRLs, simulation tools, and feasibility studies, as well as the participation in events and conferences for project dissemination.

Discussion on DC Cables and Protection Systems
Stammberger questions the conformity of DC cables to Covent OS specifications, prompting Lina Ruiz to explain the ongoing work on cable requirements and the focus on building applications. Stammberger emphasizes the expertise in the industry and suggests avoiding replication of existing work, leading to a discussion on the details of the current OS system reference documents.

Development of Protection Systems and Tools
Lucas Pereira outlines the development of semiconductor-based circuit breakers and a software tool for designing and testing protection systems in DC systems. He also discusses the development of DC-DC converters, low voltage AC and DC interlink converters, and the creation of robust measuring devices. Additionally, there is a mention of the development of a DC solutions design tool and the need for tools to simulate DC grids.
* DC Solutions and Technologies

Expert Solicitation for DC Technologies
Lucas Pereira leads the expert solicitation process, using Slido for real-time responses from participants. The questions focus on the feasibility and importance of using DC technologies in various sectors, and the answers are discussed, with emphasis on the confidence in feasibility and the varying levels of importance across different sectors.

Discussion on Materials, Tools, and Barriers to Adoption
Lucas Pereira facilitates a discussion on the significance of sustainable materials and design tools, with Stammberger and Bartonek providing insights on the importance of DC cables and design tools for the project. The team also delves into barriers to adoption, highlighting the lack of trained personnel and high costs as major challenges, while also considering the need for safety concerns to be separated from power quality issues in future discussions.
* DC Cables

Discussion on Technology Advantages and Real Scenarios
Lucas Pereira and Ceballos Recio, Salvador delve into the issue of technology advantages and real-world applications. They express the challenge of discerning the practical benefits of technology without detailed studies, and the lack of clear incentives for its use. The conversation emphasizes the need for more specific and applicable information in order to make informed decisions about technology implementation.

Discussion on Cost and Importance of Various Components
Lucas Pereira facilitates a detailed discussion on the importance and cost considerations of different components for large-scale deployment of systems. The conversation delves into the impact of cost on market traction, engineering challenges, and the lifespan of DC cables, with input from various participants such as Carlos Castro, Katharina Hetzenecker, and Lina RUIZ.

Discussion on Cost, Solutions, and Barriers
Salvador Ceballos Recio emphasizes the significance of standardization in reducing costs, while Lucas Pereira mentions the potential impact of open source solutions. Andrii Chub supports the idea of clear standards for achieving economies of scale. The discussion also covers the conditions that could positively support cost improvements.
* Timeframe for Widespread Adoption

Discussion on Widespread Adoption of Technologies
Lucas Pereira, Andrii Chub, and Katharina Hetzenecker engage in a discussion about the widespread adoption of technologies, including the availability of materials and the timeframes for deployment. They also address the need for clarity in defining static protection systems and the potential barriers to widespread adoption, such as costs, control, interoperability, and the lack of demos.
* Importance of Standards and Demos

Discussion on Risk Assessment and Tools for Project Development
Lucas Pereira led a detailed discussion on the risk assessment of project components, such as DC cables, connectors, protections, semiconductor circuit breakers, and measurement devices. The team debated the importance of smart and sustainable solutions, potential regulatory impacts, and the criticality of various tools and technologies for project development. They also explored potential breakthroughs and barriers, including interoperability issues and the need for advanced control systems and energy management solutions.
