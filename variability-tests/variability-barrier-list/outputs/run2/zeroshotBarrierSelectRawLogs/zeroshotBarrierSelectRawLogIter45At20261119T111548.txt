Based on the context provided and the role of a general expert in renewable energy and DC technologies, the following five barriers to the accelerated transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies, as per the Shift2DC project, are selected:

1. Lack of pilot projects: Insufficient demonstration and proof of concept for DC technologies can hinder their widespread adoption and investment.
2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations: Public understanding and support are crucial for the success of DC projects. If the public perceives DC technologies as risky, complex, or unnecessary, they may be less likely to support their implementation.
3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The availability of compatible components is essential for the seamless integration and efficient operation of DC systems. Inconsistent standards and component incompatibilities can lead to increased costs and reduced system performance.
4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Misunderstandings and insufficient knowledge about DC technologies can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, which can discourage the adoption of these technologies.
5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The development and implementation of DC technologies require specialized skills. A shortage of trained personnel can slow down the growth and deployment of these technologies.

These barriers are not ranked in order of importance, as the focus of the Shift2DC project is on addressing all identified challenges to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.
