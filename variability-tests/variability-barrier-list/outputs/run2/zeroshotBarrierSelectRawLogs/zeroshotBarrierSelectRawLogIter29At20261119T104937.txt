3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization in DC systems components can lead to compatibility issues, which can increase costs and complexity during installation and maintenance.
4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: A lack of familiarity with DC technology among designers, engineers, and permitting authorities can result in lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, slowing down the adoption of DC solutions.
5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The limited number of skilled professionals in DC systems can make it difficult to find qualified personnel to design, install, and maintain DC systems, which can increase costs and slow down the adoption of these technologies.
