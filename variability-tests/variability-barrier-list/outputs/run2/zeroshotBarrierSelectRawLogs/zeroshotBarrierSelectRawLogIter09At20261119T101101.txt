Based on the provided context, I would select the following five barriers for the Shift2DC project:

1. Lack of pilot projects: The absence of pilot projects poses a significant barrier to the acceleration of DC technology adoption. Without successful demonstrations, it is challenging to build confidence in the technology and attract investment.

2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: A positive public perception is crucial for the widespread adoption of any new technology. If people are not aware of or do not understand the benefits of DC technologies, they may be less likely to support or invest in them.

3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Ensuring compatibility between different components of DC systems is essential for seamless integration and efficient operation. Incompatibilities can lead to increased costs, delays, and reduced performance.

4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Misconceptions and lack of knowledge about DC technologies can result in lengthy and expensive design and permit processes. This barrier can be addressed through education and outreach to help stakeholders understand the advantages of DC technologies and the requirements for their implementation.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: As the adoption of DC technologies increases, there will be a growing need for skilled professionals who can design, install, and maintain these systems. A lack of trained personnel can slow down the transition to DC technologies and increase costs.
