Based on the provided context and my expertise, I select the following five barriers for the Shift2DC project:

1. Lack of pilot projects: The absence of existing pilot projects makes it challenging to demonstrate the practicality and effectiveness of DC technologies, potentially discouraging investments and widespread adoption.
2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: A negative or uninformed public perception can hinder the acceptance and adoption of DC technologies, making it difficult to secure support and funding for new installations.
3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization and compatibility among DC systems components can create difficulties in integrating various technologies, leading to higher costs and longer implementation times.
4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Inadequate understanding of DC technologies can result in complex and costly design and permit processes, deterring potential adopters and increasing the financial burden of transitioning to DC systems.
5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: A shortage of skilled professionals with expertise in DC technologies can create bottlenecks in the development, installation, and maintenance of DC systems, potentially slowing down the transition to DC energy systems.
