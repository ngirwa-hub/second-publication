3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Ensuring compatibility between various DC components is essential for seamless integration and efficient operation. Incompatibility can lead to increased costs and potential system failures.

4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: A lack of understanding about DC technologies can result in more complex and expensive design and permit processes, potentially slowing down the adoption of these solutions.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: As DC technologies become more prevalent, there is a growing need for skilled professionals who can design, install, and maintain these systems. A shortage of trained personnel can slow down the transition to DC technologies.
