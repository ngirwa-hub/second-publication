4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Inadequate understanding of DC technologies can result in unnecessarily lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, making the adoption of DC technologies less attractive compared to traditional AC-based systems.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The dearth of skilled workers familiar with DC technologies can make it challenging to design, install, and maintain DC systems, which can hinder the widespread adoption of these technologies.

These barriers are crucial to address for the successful implementation of the Shift2DC project and the wider promotion of DC technologies in various sectors.
