3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Compatibility issues between various DC components can lead to higher costs, longer installation times, and reduced system efficiency.

4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: A lack of understanding of DC technologies among designers, engineers, and permitting authorities can result in lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, making DC solutions less attractive compared to traditional AC systems.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: A shortage of skilled workers with expertise in DC technologies can create bottlenecks in the deployment and maintenance of DC systems, ultimately slowing down the transition to a sustainable energy system.
