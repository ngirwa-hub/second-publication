--- mistral_generalist ---
Based on the context provided, here are the five barriers I would select for the Shift2DC project:

1. Lack of pilot projects: The absence of existing demonstrations and case studies of DC technologies can hinder their adoption and make it difficult for stakeholders to evaluate their benefits and drawbacks.

2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: A limited understanding of DC technologies among the general public and decision-makers can slow down the adoption of DC solutions, as there may be a reluctance to invest in new technologies that are not well understood.

3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization and interoperability among DC components can lead to compatibility issues, making it challenging to design and implement DC systems efficiently.

4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Inadequate understanding of DC technologies can lead to longer and more costly design and permit processes, potentially increasing the overall cost of implementing DC solutions.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: A shortage of skilled workers with expertise in DC technologies can make it difficult to design, install, and maintain DC systems, potentially slowing down their adoption and increasing their cost.

These barriers were chosen as they are directly related to the promotion and acceleration of the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies, as outlined in the Shift2DC project.
