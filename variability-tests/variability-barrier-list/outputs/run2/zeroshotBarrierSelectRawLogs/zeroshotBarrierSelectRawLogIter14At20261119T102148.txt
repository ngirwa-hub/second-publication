Based on my expertise, I would select the following five barriers to the accelerated transition to a sustainable energy system through the promotion of Direct Current (DC) technologies, as part of the Shift2DC project:

1. Lack of pilot projects: Insufficient demonstration of DC solutions can hinder the development of best practices, prove the technology's viability, and build public confidence.

2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: A positive public image is crucial for the widespread adoption of DC technologies. However, the public's understanding and acceptance of DC systems may still be limited.

3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Interoperability issues can arise when different DC components are not designed to work together seamlessly, which can increase costs and complexity.

4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Unfamiliarity with DC systems can result in longer and more costly design and permit processes, which may deter potential adopters.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: A shortage of skilled professionals in DC technologies can slow down the deployment of DC solutions and increase costs.

These barriers are critical to address in order to facilitate the transition to a sustainable energy system through the promotion of DC technologies.
