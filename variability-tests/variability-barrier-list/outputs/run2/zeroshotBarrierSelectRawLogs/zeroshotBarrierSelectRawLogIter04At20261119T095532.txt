1. Lack of pilot projects: The absence of well-documented pilot projects can hinder the adoption of DC technologies, as there is a lack of evidence demonstrating their effectiveness and feasibility in real-world applications.

2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: A negative or indifferent public perception towards DC technologies can slow down their adoption, as there may be a lack of understanding and support for these innovations.

3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization and interoperability among various DC systems components can create challenges in integrating and deploying DC solutions, potentially leading to increased costs and complexity.

4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Inadequate understanding and knowledge of DC technologies can result in longer and more expensive design and permit processes, which can deter potential adopters due to increased financial burdens and delays.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: Insufficient human resources skilled in DC technologies can hinder the implementation and maintenance of DC systems, as there may not be enough experts available to design, install, and troubleshoot these systems effectively.

These five barriers are particularly relevant to the Shift2DC project, as they directly impact the development and demonstration of DC solutions in the targeted sectors. Addressing these challenges will be crucial for the successful implementation and widespread adoption of DC technologies.
