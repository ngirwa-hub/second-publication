2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: A positive public perception is crucial for the widespread adoption of any new technology. Educating the public about the benefits and advantages of DC technologies can help foster support and encourage more individuals and organizations to adopt DC solutions.

Improving awareness and understanding of DC systems among designers, engineers, and permitting authorities can help streamline the process and reduce costs.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The deployment of DC technologies requires specialized knowledge and skills. Investing in training programs and education initiatives can help address the shortage of trained personnel and ensure the proper installation, maintenance, and operation of DC systems.

These five barriers represent significant challenges that need to be addressed to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.
