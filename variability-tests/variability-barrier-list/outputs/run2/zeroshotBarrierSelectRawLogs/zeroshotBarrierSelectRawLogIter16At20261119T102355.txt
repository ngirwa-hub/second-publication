3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization and compatibility among various DC system components can create challenges during integration and installation, potentially leading to increased costs and longer project timelines.

4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Misunderstandings and insufficient knowledge about DC technologies can result in time-consuming and costly design and permit processes, delaying the implementation of projects.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: A shortage of skilled professionals with expertise in DC technologies can make it difficult to implement and maintain DC projects, potentially leading to increased costs and reduced efficiency.

These barriers highlight the importance of addressing knowledge gaps, fostering public engagement, promoting standardization, and developing a skilled workforce to support the transition to a more sustainable energy system using DC technologies.
