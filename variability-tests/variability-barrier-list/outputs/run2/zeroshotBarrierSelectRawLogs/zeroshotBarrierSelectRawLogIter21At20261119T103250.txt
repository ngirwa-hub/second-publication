1. Lack of pilot projects: The absence of existing pilot projects could hinder the progress of DC technologies, as they provide valuable data for understanding their performance and potential.

2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations: Addressing public perception and gaining acceptance for DC technologies is crucial for accelerating their adoption. A positive public image can drive investment and support for DC projects.

3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Ensuring compatibility among various DC system components is essential for seamless integration and efficient operation of DC systems.

4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Incorrect assumptions and insufficient knowledge about DC technologies can lead to complicated and expensive design and permit processes, potentially slowing down the adoption of these technologies.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: As DC technologies become more widespread, there is a growing need for skilled professionals with expertise in DC systems design, installation, and maintenance. A shortage of trained personnel could impede the transition to DC technologies.
