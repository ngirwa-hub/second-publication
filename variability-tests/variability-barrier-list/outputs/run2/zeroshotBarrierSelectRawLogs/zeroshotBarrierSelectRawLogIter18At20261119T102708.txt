These five barriers have been selected as they present significant challenges in the transition to a sustainable energy system using direct current (DC) technologies. The absence of pilot projects hinders the demonstration of DC solutions and their effectiveness. Public perception and readiness to support DC projects are crucial for their widespread adoption. Incompatibility of DC systems components can lead to technical challenges and increased costs. Misconceptions and lack of knowledge can prolong the design and permit process, leading to increased costs and delays. Lastly, a lack of trained personnel in DC systems can hinder the implementation and maintenance of these technologies.
