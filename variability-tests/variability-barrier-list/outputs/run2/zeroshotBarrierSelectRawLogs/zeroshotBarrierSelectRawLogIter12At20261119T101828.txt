Based on the provided context and my role as a subject-matter expert in DC technologies, I would select the following five barriers from the list for the Shift2DC project:

1. Lack of pilot projects: The absence of pilot projects demonstrating the practicality and effectiveness of DC technologies can hinder the adoption of these solutions.

2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: A general lack of understanding and acceptance of DC technologies among the public can slow down the progress of DC projects.

3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization and interoperability between DC system components can lead to compatibility issues and increased costs.

4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Limited understanding of DC technologies can result in lengthy and costly design and permit processes, potentially slowing down the deployment of DC solutions.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The scarcity of skilled professionals knowledgeable in DC technologies can create challenges in the design, installation, and maintenance of DC systems.

These five barriers were selected due to their direct impact on the implementation and adoption of DC technologies in the four sectors focused by the Shift2DC project (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings). Addressing these barriers can help accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.
