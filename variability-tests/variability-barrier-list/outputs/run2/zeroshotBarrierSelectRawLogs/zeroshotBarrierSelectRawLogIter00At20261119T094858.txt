These selected barriers present significant challenges for the adoption and implementation of direct current (DC) technologies in various sectors, as outlined by the Shift2DC project. By addressing these obstacles, we can accelerate the transition to a more sustainable energy system.

1. Lack of pilot projects: The absence of proven DC solutions in real-world settings can make it difficult for stakeholders to assess their feasibility and effectiveness.
2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: A lack of understanding or acceptance of DC technology among the general public can hinder its adoption and implementation.
3. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Inadequate understanding of DC technology can lead to delays and increased costs during the design and permitting phases of projects.
4. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: A shortage of skilled professionals familiar with DC technologies can create challenges in the design, installation, and maintenance of DC systems.
5. High costs of DC solutions: The initial costs of implementing DC technologies can be a barrier to their widespread adoption, particularly in sectors where budgets are limited.
6. Reduced reliability in DC devices: Concerns about the reliability and longevity of DC devices, compared to their alternating current (AC) counterparts, may discourage some stakeholders from adopting DC technologies.

By addressing these barriers, we can help pave the way for a more efficient, sustainable, and cost-effective energy system powered by direct current (DC) technologies.

4.
