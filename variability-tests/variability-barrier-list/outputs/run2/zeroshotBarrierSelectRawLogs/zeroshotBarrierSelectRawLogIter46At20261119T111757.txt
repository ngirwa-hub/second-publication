4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: A lack of familiarity with DC technologies among designers, planners, and regulatory bodies can result in lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, which can deter potential investors and project implementers.

5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The deployment and maintenance of DC systems require specialized knowledge and skills. A shortage of trained personnel can lead to delays, inefficiencies, and increased costs in the implementation and operation of DC projects.
