2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: A positive public perception is crucial for the successful deployment of renewable energy solutions. If the public is not well-informed or not ready to support DC projects, it can slow down their implementation.
3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Compatibility issues between various DC system components can lead to technical challenges and higher costs during installation and maintenance.
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: A clear and supportive regulatory environment is essential for the growth of any new technology. Uncertainty in regulations can deter investments and hinder the development of DC technologies.
5. High costs of DC solutions: Although DC technologies have several advantages, they can still be more expensive compared to AC solutions. High costs can be a significant barrier for widespread adoption, especially in the early stages of technology development.

These five barriers are crucial to address in order to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies, as outlined by the Shift2DC project.
