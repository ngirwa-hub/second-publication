--- mistral_generalist ---
Based on the provided context and my expert role, I would select the following five barriers from the list:

1. Lack of pilot projects: The absence of pilot projects hinders the practical demonstration and validation of DC technologies, which can discourage potential investors and users.
2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: A lack of understanding and acceptance among the public can lead to resistance towards adopting DC technologies, making it challenging to gain support and investment for new projects.
3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Compatibility issues between different DC system components can lead to increased costs and complexity in system design, installation, and maintenance.
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Unclear or inconsistent regulations can create uncertainty for investors and project developers, making it difficult to plan and implement DC projects effectively.
5. High costs of DC solutions: The initial investment costs for DC technologies can be higher compared to traditional AC systems, which may deter potential adopters and slow down the transition to DC technologies.
