Based on the provided context, here are five barriers that I would select for the Shift2DC project:

1. Lack of pilot projects: The absence of large-scale demonstration projects can hinder the acceptance and adoption of DC technologies.
2. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: A lack of understanding and support from the public can be a significant barrier to the widespread implementation of DC technologies.
3. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization and compatibility between different DC system components can lead to increased costs and complexity during installation and operation.
4. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Misunderstandings about DC technologies can result in prolonged and costly design and permit processes, slowing down the transition to DC systems.
5. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: A shortage of skilled professionals knowledgeable in DC technologies can make it challenging to implement and maintain DC systems effectively.

By addressing these barriers, the Shift2DC project can help facilitate the transition to a more sustainable energy system through the promotion and development of DC technologies.
