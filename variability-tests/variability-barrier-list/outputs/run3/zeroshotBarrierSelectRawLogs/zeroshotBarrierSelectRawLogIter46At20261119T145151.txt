4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they directly impact the adoption and implementation of DC technologies in various sectors, as outlined by the Shift2DC project. They represent significant challenges that need to be addressed to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system through DC technologies.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: This barrier arises due to the lack of standardization among DC components, which can lead to compatibility issues, increasing costs and complexity in system design and implementation.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Misunderstanding of DC technologies can result in longer and more expensive design and permit processes, as regulatory bodies and permitting authorities may not be familiar with the specific requirements of DC systems.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: As DC technologies are still emerging, there is a need for a skilled workforce to design, install, and maintain DC systems. The lack of trained personnel can slow down the adoption and implementation of DC technologies.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Unclear regulations and standards for DC technologies can create uncertainty for investors, project developers, and manufacturers, making it difficult to plan and implement DC projects.

5. High costs of DC solutions: While DC technologies offer several benefits, such as reduced energy losses and improved efficiency, the initial investment costs can be higher compared to AC systems. This barrier can deter potential adopters of DC technologies.

These barriers, if addressed, can help facilitate a smoother transition to a sustainable energy system through the use of DC technologies.
