--- mistral_generalist ---
1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they present significant challenges in the transition to a sustainable energy system using direct current (DC) technologies. The incompatibility of DC systems components can hinder the efficient integration of various components in a DC system. Misconceptions and lack of knowledge can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, which can deter the adoption of DC technologies. The lack of trained personnel in DC systems can slow down the implementation and maintenance of DC systems. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and developers, and high costs of DC solutions can make them less attractive compared to traditional AC systems. These barriers need to be addressed to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.
