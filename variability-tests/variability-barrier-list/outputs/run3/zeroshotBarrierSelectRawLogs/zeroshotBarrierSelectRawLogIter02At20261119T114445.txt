1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they directly impact the transition to a sustainable energy system through the promotion of direct current (DC) technologies, as per the context of the Shift2DC project.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components hinders the seamless integration of various DC components in a system, leading to increased complexity and costs.
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes due to unfamiliarity with DC technologies and systems.
3. Lack of trained personnel in DC systems can cause delays in project implementation and increased costs for training new staff.
4. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and project developers, making it difficult to plan and implement DC projects.
5. High costs of DC solutions can make it challenging to compete with traditional AC systems, making it difficult to drive widespread adoption of DC technologies.

These barriers require attention and solutions to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.
