These five barriers are significant obstacles to the widespread adoption of DC technologies. Incompatibility of DC systems components can lead to technical issues and increased costs. A lack of trained personnel in DC systems can hinder the implementation and maintenance of DC systems. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and project developers.
