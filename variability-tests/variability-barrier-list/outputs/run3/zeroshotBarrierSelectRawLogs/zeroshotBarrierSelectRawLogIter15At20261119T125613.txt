--- mistral_subject-matter ---
1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they pose significant challenges to the widespread adoption of DC technologies in various sectors, including ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. The incompatibility of DC systems components, lack of knowledge, and insufficient trained personnel can lead to increased costs and complexities during design and permit processes. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can also contribute to delays and uncertainties in the adoption of DC technologies. The high costs of DC solutions, compared to traditional AC-based systems, can act as a deterrent for widespread adoption, and the lack of pilots projects and use-cases demonstrating the advantages of DC technologies may further hinder their adoption.
