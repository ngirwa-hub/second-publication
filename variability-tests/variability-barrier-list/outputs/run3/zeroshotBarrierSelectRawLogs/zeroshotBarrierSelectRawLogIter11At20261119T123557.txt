1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: This is a significant issue as it can hinder the seamless integration and operation of various DC components, leading to increased costs and potential system failures.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: This barrier is crucial because it can result in unnecessary delays and expenses during the planning and approval stages of DC projects, potentially slowing down the transition to a more sustainable energy system.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The absence of skilled professionals in DC technologies can lead to inefficiencies, errors, and increased costs during the installation, operation, and maintenance of DC systems.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Regulatory uncertainty can create obstacles for the development and implementation of DC technologies, as it may lead to potential legal and financial risks for project developers and investors.

5. High costs of DC solutions: The higher initial costs associated with DC technologies compared to AC solutions can be a significant barrier to their widespread adoption, particularly in the early stages of the transition to a more sustainable energy system.

By addressing these five barriers, the Shift2DC project can work towards accelerating the transition to a sustainable energy system by promoting the use of DC technologies more effectively.
