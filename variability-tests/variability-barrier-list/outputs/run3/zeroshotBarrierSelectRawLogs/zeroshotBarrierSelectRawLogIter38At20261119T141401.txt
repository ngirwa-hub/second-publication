--- mistral_generalist ---
Based on the provided context and my role as an expert in renewable energy and DC technologies, I would select the following five barriers from the list:

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The diversity and lack of standardization in DC components can lead to compatibility issues, making it difficult to integrate various systems effectively.
2. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The relatively new adoption of DC technologies compared to AC technologies means there is a shortage of skilled professionals who are familiar with DC system design, installation, and maintenance.
3. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: The evolving regulatory landscape can create uncertainty for investors and developers, making it challenging to plan and execute DC projects due to unclear regulations and standards.
4. High costs of DC solutions: The initial investment costs for DC technologies can be higher than those for AC systems, which can deter adoption and slow down the transition to a DC-based energy system.
5. Lack of pilot projects: A lack of successful pilot projects demonstrating the benefits and feasibility of DC technologies can hinder widespread adoption and make it difficult to attract investors and stakeholders.

These barriers, if addressed, could help accelerate the transition to a more sustainable DC-based energy system as part of the Shift2DC project.
