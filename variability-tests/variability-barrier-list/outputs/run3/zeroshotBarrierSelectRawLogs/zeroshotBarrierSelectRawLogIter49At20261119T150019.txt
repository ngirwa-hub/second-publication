These five barriers are significant challenges in the transition to a sustainable energy system using direct current (DC) technologies, as outlined in the Shift2DC project.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The diversity and complexity of DC systems components can lead to incompatibility issues, hindering the widespread adoption of DC technologies.
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Limited understanding of DC technologies can result in lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, creating barriers to DC implementation.
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The limited availability of trained professionals with expertise in DC systems can hinder the development and implementation of DC projects.
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: The absence of clear regulations and standards for DC technologies can create uncertainty and impede the progress of DC projects.
5. High costs of DC solutions: The higher initial costs of DC solutions compared to alternating current (AC) systems can be a significant barrier to their adoption, particularly in the context of renewable energy integration.

These barriers necessitate focused attention and targeted solutions to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.
