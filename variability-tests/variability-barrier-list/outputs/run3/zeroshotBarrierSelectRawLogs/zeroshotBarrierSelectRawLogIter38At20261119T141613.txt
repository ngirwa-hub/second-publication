4. High costs of DC solutions: The upfront costs of DC solutions can be a significant barrier to their widespread adoption. High costs can deter potential investors and users, making it challenging to scale up the deployment of DC technologies.

5. Lack of pilot projects: Pilot projects are essential for demonstrating the viability and advantages of new technologies. The absence of pilot projects can hinder the development and adoption of DC technologies, as they provide valuable data and insights for potential investors and users.

These five barriers are crucial to address in order to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system through the promotion of DC technologies.
