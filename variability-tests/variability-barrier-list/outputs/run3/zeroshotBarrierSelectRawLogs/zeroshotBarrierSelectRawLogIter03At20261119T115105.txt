3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they present significant challenges in accelerating the transition to a sustainable energy system by promoting the use of direct current (DC) technologies, as outlined in the Shift2DC project.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization and compatibility between different DC components can lead to increased costs and complexity during installation and operation.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: A lack of understanding about DC technologies can result in lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, as officials may not be familiar with the specific requirements and regulations for DC systems.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The limited availability of skilled professionals familiar with DC technologies can slow down the adoption and implementation of DC solutions in various sectors.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Unclear or inconsistent regulations related to DC technologies can create uncertainty for investors, developers, and manufacturers, making it difficult to invest in and scale up DC projects.

5. High costs of DC solutions: The initial cost of DC solutions can be higher than AC solutions, making it challenging for projects to compete with established, more affordable technologies in the market.
