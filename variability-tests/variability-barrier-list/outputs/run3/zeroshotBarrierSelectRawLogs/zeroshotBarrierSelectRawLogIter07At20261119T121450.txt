These five barriers were selected based on their potential impact on the widespread adoption of DC technologies in the four sectors mentioned in the Shift2DC project context. The selected barriers address technical, regulatory, and societal challenges that need to be addressed to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization and compatibility among DC components can lead to increased costs and complexity in system design, installation, and maintenance.
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Misunderstandings about DC technologies can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, which can slow down the adoption of DC technologies.
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: A shortage of trained personnel with expertise in DC technologies can hinder the development and deployment of DC solutions, as well as increase costs due to a reliance on external consultants.
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Unclear regulations and a lack of agreed standards can create uncertainty and increase costs for developers and investors in DC projects.
5. High costs of DC solutions: The cost of DC solutions can be a barrier to their widespread adoption, as they may be more expensive than AC solutions in some applications. Reducing costs and increasing efficiency will be crucial for the widespread adoption of DC technologies.
