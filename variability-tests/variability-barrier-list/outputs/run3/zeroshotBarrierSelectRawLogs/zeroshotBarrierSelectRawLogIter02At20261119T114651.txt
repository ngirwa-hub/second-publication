3. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
4. High costs of DC solutions
5. Lack of pilot projects

These five barriers have been selected as they directly impact the adoption and implementation of DC technologies in various sectors, such as ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. The incompatibility of DC systems components can lead to technical challenges and increased costs. The lack of trained personnel in DC systems can hinder the efficient design, installation, and maintenance of DC systems. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and stakeholders, making it difficult to plan and implement DC projects. High costs of DC solutions can be a significant barrier to their adoption, particularly in the early stages of the transition to a sustainable energy system. Lastly, the absence of pilot projects can limit the availability of real-world examples and case studies, making it difficult to demonstrate the benefits of DC technologies and build confidence in their use.
