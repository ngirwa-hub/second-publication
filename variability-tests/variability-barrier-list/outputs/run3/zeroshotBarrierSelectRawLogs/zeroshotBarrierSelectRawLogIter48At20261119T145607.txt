1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they directly impact the adoption and implementation of direct current (DC) technologies in various sectors, as outlined in the Shift2DC project.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The absence of standardized components for DC power systems can lead to compatibility issues, making it challenging to design and integrate DC solutions seamlessly.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: A lack of understanding about DC technologies can result in lengthy and expensive design and permit processes due to the need for extensive justification and additional safety measures.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: As DC technologies become more prevalent, there is a growing need for skilled professionals who can design, install, and maintain DC power systems. However, the current workforce may not be adequately equipped to handle these tasks.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Regulatory uncertainty can discourage investments in DC technologies, as it can create obstacles in the deployment and operation of DC systems.

5. High costs of DC solutions: The initial costs associated with DC solutions can be a significant barrier to their widespread adoption, especially in comparison to traditional AC systems.

The remaining barriers, while important, have been left unselected to ensure a diverse representation of challenges facing the adoption of DC technologies in the Shift2DC project's focus sectors.
