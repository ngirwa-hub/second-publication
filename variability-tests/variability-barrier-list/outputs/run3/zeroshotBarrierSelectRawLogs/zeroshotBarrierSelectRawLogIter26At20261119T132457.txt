--- mistral_subject-matter ---
1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers are selected based on their significant impact on the adoption and implementation of DC technologies in various sectors, as outlined in the Shift2DC project context.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The diversity and proprietary nature of components in DC systems can lead to incompatibility issues, which can impact interoperability and increase costs.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Limited understanding of DC technologies can result in longer and more expensive design and permit processes due to unfamiliarity with system requirements and standards.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The relatively small scale of DC technology adoption compared to AC technology has resulted in a shortage of skilled professionals who are knowledgeable in DC systems design, operation, and maintenance.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Regulatory uncertainty can create barriers to the widespread adoption of DC technologies, as it makes it difficult for stakeholders to make long-term investment decisions.

5. High costs of DC solutions: The initial costs of DC technologies can be higher compared to AC solutions, which can deter potential adopters, especially in the early stages of technology adoption.
