1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization in DC components and systems can lead to compatibility issues, making it difficult to integrate and optimize DC technologies.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: The unfamiliarity of regulators and professionals with DC technologies can result in lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, hindering the adoption of DC solutions.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The scarcity of skilled workers with expertise in DC technologies can cause delays and increase costs in the implementation of DC projects.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: The absence of clear regulations and guidelines for DC systems can create uncertainty and hinder investments in DC technologies.

5. High costs of DC solutions: The higher upfront costs of DC technologies compared to traditional AC systems can deter potential investors and slow down the transition to DC-based renewable energy systems.

These barriers need to be addressed to accelerate the adoption of DC technologies and achieve the goals of the Shift2DC project.
