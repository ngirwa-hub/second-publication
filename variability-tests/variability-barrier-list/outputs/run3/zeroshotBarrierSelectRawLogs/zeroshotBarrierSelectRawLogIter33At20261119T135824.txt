--- mistral_generalist ---
1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers are significant challenges that the Shift2DC project aims to address in promoting the use of direct current (DC) technologies. The incompatibility of DC systems components is a technical issue that can hinder the seamless integration of DC technologies. The misconception and lack of knowledge about DC systems can lead to longer and more expensive design and permit processes, potentially slowing down the adoption of DC technologies. A lack of trained personnel in DC systems can make it difficult to implement and maintain these technologies effectively. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and developers, potentially deterring them from investing in DC technologies. Lastly, the high costs of DC solutions can be a significant barrier to widespread adoption, as they may not be cost-competitive with traditional AC systems in certain applications.
