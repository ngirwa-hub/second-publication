--- mistral_subject-matter ---
1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These barriers have been selected because they directly impact the adoption and implementation of DC technologies in various sectors. Incompatibility of components can hinder the seamless integration of DC systems, while lack of knowledge can lead to design and permit process delays and increased costs. The absence of trained personnel can slow down the deployment and maintenance of DC systems. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and project developers, while high costs can make DC solutions less attractive compared to AC alternatives. These barriers need to be addressed to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system through DC technologies as envisioned by the Shift2DC project.
