--- mistral_subject-matter ---
Based on the context and my role as a subject-matter expert in DC technologies, the following five barriers are selected:

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The diverse range of DC systems components, coupled with a lack of standardization, can lead to compatibility issues that may increase costs and complexity during system design and installation.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Misconceptions and limited knowledge about DC technologies among designers, permitting authorities, and other stakeholders can result in lengthy and expensive design and permit processes due to unfamiliarity with DC-specific requirements.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The relatively new and evolving nature of DC technologies creates a demand for trained personnel with specialized knowledge in this area. The current shortage of such professionals can hinder the widespread adoption of DC systems.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: The absence of clear and consistent regulations for DC technologies can create uncertainty for stakeholders, making it difficult to plan and invest in DC projects.

5. High costs of DC solutions: The cost of DC solutions, particularly in comparison to AC solutions, can be a significant barrier to widespread adoption. High costs may be due to factors such as the need for specialized components, increased complexity, and the relatively low volume of DC technology manufacturing compared to AC technologies.
