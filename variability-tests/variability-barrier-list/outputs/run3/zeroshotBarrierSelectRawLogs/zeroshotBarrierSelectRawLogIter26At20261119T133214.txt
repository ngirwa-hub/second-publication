1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they directly impact the acceleration of the transition to a sustainable energy system by promoting the use of direct current (DC) technologies. Addressing these challenges is crucial for overcoming obstacles in the adoption of DC technologies in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: This barrier slows down the transition to DC technologies due to the lack of standardization and interoperability among various DC components, making it challenging to implement seamless and efficient DC systems.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Misinformation or a lack of understanding about DC technologies can lead to prolonged and costly design and permit processes, causing delays in the deployment of DC systems.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The scarcity of skilled professionals familiar with DC technologies hinders the growth and adoption of these systems, as there is a need for a larger workforce to support the design, installation, and maintenance of DC solutions.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: An unclear regulatory landscape can create uncertainty for investors and stakeholders, making it difficult to plan and invest in DC projects, which can hamper the transition to a sustainable energy system.

5. High costs of DC solutions: The higher upfront costs associated with DC technologies compared to alternating current (AC) systems can be a significant barrier to their adoption, particularly for smaller projects and organizations with limited budgets.

By addressing these five barriers, the Shift2DC project can help pave the way for a more efficient, sustainable, and widespread adoption of direct current technologies in various sectors.
