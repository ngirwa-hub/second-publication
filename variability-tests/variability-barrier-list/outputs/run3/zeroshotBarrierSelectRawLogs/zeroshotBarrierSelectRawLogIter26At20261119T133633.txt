--- mistral_generalist ---
Based on the context of the Shift2DC project and my role as an expert in renewable energy and DC technologies, I would select the following five barriers:

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The diversity of DC systems components can lead to compatibility issues, making it difficult to integrate different devices and technologies efficiently.

2. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: As DC technologies are still not as widespread as AC systems, there is a shortage of skilled professionals who can design, install, and maintain DC systems.

3. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Regulations and standards for DC systems are not yet well-established in many regions, creating uncertainty for businesses and investors in the sector.

4. High costs of DC solutions: The initial cost of implementing DC technologies can be higher than AC solutions, making it a barrier for widespread adoption, particularly in the early stages of market development.

5. Uncertain utility interaction (net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards): The relationship between DC system owners and utility companies is not always clear, with questions remaining about net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards, which can create obstacles for the expansion of DC technologies.
