1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The incompatibility between various DC components can hinder the seamless integration and scalability of DC systems, making it challenging to promote widespread adoption.

2. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: A shortage of skilled professionals familiar with DC technologies can lead to installation and maintenance issues, potentially slowing down the adoption of DC solutions.

3. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: A clear and consistent regulatory framework is essential for the growth of any new technology. Uncertainty in regulations can discourage investments and slow down the transition to DC technologies.

4. High costs of DC solutions: The initial investment costs for DC technologies can be a significant barrier, as they may be more expensive compared to traditional AC systems. Reducing costs and providing economic incentives is crucial to encourage the adoption of DC technologies.

5. Lack of pilot projects: Successful pilot projects demonstrate the feasibility and benefits of new technologies, helping to build confidence and momentum. The absence of pilot projects can hinder the growth of the DC technology sector.

By addressing these five barriers, the Shift2DC project can contribute to overcoming challenges and accelerating the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.
