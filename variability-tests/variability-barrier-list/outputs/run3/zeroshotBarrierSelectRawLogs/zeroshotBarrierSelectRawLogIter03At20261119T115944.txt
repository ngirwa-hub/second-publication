--- mistral_subject-matter ---
1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they pose significant challenges to the widespread adoption of DC technologies in various sectors, such as ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. Addressing these barriers will be crucial in accelerating the transition to a sustainable energy system promoted by the Shift2DC project.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The diversity of DC systems components can lead to compatibility issues, which may increase costs and complexity in implementing DC solutions.
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Misunderstandings about DC technologies can result in longer and more expensive design and permit processes, slowing down the adoption of these technologies.
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: A shortage of trained professionals familiar with DC technologies can hinder the development, installation, and maintenance of DC systems.
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: An unclear regulatory environment can create uncertainty for investors and developers, making it difficult to plan and implement DC projects.
5. High costs of DC solutions: The higher costs of DC solutions compared to alternating current (AC) systems can be a significant barrier to their widespread adoption, particularly in the early stages of market penetration.
