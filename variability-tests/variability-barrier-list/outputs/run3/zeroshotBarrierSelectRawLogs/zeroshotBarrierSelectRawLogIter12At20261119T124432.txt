2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers are particularly significant in the context of the Shift2DC project, which aims to promote the use of DC technologies in various sectors. The incompatibility of DC systems components is a major challenge, as it can hinder the integration of different DC technologies and devices. Misconceptions and lack of knowledge can lead to complex and expensive design and permit processes, adding unnecessary costs and delays. The shortage of trained personnel in DC systems can slow down the adoption and implementation of these technologies. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and developers, making it harder to plan and execute projects. Lastly, the high costs of DC solutions can make it difficult for these technologies to compete with traditional AC solutions, and may deter adoption in certain sectors.
