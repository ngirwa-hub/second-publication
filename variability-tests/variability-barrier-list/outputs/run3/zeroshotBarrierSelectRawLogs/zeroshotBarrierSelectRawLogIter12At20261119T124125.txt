3. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: A clear and consistent regulatory framework is essential for the adoption of new technologies. However, the uncertainty surrounding regulations can create barriers for investors and manufacturers, slowing down the transition to DC technologies.

4. High costs of DC solutions: Although DC technologies offer potential advantages in terms of efficiency and energy savings, the initial costs can be higher than traditional AC solutions. This financial barrier can discourage the adoption of DC technologies, particularly in the early stages of implementation.

5. Lack of pilot projects: The successful demonstration of DC technologies through pilot projects can help to build confidence and encourage wider adoption. However, the absence of sufficient pilot projects can create a barrier to the development and validation of DC solutions.
