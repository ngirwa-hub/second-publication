--- mistral_subject-matter ---
1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they present significant challenges in the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies, as outlined in the Shift2DC project context.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components can lead to integration issues, making it difficult to implement and scale up DC solutions.
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes due to a lack of understanding of DC technology among industry professionals and regulators.
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems can slow down the adoption and implementation of DC technologies, as there may not be enough experts available to design, install, and maintain DC systems.
4. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and project developers, making it difficult to plan and implement DC projects.
5. High costs of DC solutions can make it difficult for DC technologies to compete with traditional AC systems, particularly in the early stages of deployment.
