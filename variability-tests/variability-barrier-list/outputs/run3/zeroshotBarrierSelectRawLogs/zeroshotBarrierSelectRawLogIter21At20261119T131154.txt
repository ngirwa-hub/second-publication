1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers are significant challenges in the transition to a sustainable energy system that promotes the use of direct current (DC) technologies. Incompatibility of DC systems components can hinder the efficient integration of various DC components in a system. Misconceptions and lack of knowledge can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, which can discourage the adoption of DC technologies. The lack of trained personnel in DC systems can slow down the implementation and maintenance of DC projects. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and developers, making it difficult to plan and execute DC projects. Lastly, high costs of DC solutions can make them less attractive to potential adopters compared to traditional AC systems.
