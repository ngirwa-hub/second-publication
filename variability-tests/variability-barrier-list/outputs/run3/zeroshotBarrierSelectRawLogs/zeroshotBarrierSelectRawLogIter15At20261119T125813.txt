--- mistral_generalist ---
1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they represent significant challenges in the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Different DC components may not be compatible, which can lead to integration issues and increased costs.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Limited understanding of DC technology among stakeholders can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The lack of skilled workers familiar with DC technology can hinder the implementation and maintenance of DC systems.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Unclear regulations can create uncertainty for investors and developers, making it difficult to plan and implement DC projects.

5. High costs of DC solutions: Higher initial costs for DC solutions compared to AC systems can be a barrier to their widespread adoption.

These barriers, if addressed, can contribute to a smoother transition to a sustainable energy system utilizing DC technologies as part of the Shift2DC project.
