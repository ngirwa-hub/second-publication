--- mistral_subject-matter ---
1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they pose significant challenges in the widespread adoption of DC technologies, particularly in the context of the Shift2DC project. Incompatibility of DC systems components can lead to technical difficulties and increased costs. Misconception and lack of knowledge can lead to complex and costly design and permit processes. Lack of trained personnel can hinder the implementation and maintenance of DC systems. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and project developers. High costs of DC solutions can make them less attractive compared to traditional AC systems. These barriers need to be addressed to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.
