1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they directly impact the adoption and implementation of direct current (DC) technologies in various sectors, as outlined in the Shift2DC project. Incompatibility of DC systems components can hinder the seamless integration of new DC equipment into existing systems. Lack of knowledge and misconceptions can lead to costly and time-consuming design and permit processes. A shortage of trained personnel in DC systems may slow down the development and deployment of DC solutions. Uncertain regulatory roadmaps create unpredictability for stakeholders, while high costs of DC solutions can make them less attractive compared to traditional alternatives. These barriers need to be addressed to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.
