2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they directly impact the implementation and adoption of DC technologies in various sectors. Incompatibility of DC systems components can hinder the integration of different DC components, leading to technical challenges. Misconception and lack of knowledge can result in longer and more expensive design and permit processes, potentially deterring potential adopters. A lack of trained personnel in DC systems can slow down the deployment and maintenance of DC technologies. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and project developers, potentially hindering the adoption of DC technologies. Lastly, high costs of DC solutions can make them less attractive compared to traditional AC systems, making it challenging to compete in the market.
