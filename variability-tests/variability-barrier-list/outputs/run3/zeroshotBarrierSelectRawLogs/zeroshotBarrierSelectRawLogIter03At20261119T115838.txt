3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers are significant obstacles to the widespread adoption of direct current (DC) technologies. The incompatibility of DC systems components can lead to technical challenges and increased costs. Misconceptions and lack of knowledge can result in lengthy and expensive design and permit processes. A lack of trained personnel in DC systems can hinder the implementation and maintenance of these technologies. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and developers. Lastly, the high costs of DC solutions can deter adoption, especially in the early stages of market development. Addressing these barriers is crucial for the successful transition to a sustainable energy system through the promotion of DC technologies.
