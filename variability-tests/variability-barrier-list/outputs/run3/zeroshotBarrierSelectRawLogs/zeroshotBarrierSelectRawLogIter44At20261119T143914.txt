--- mistral_generalist ---
1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers are the most significant challenges in accelerating the transition to a sustainable energy system using direct current (DC) technologies, as outlined in the Shift2DC project.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The current energy infrastructure predominantly uses alternating current (AC). As a result, integrating DC systems with existing infrastructure can be difficult due to compatibility issues.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Limited understanding of DC technologies can lead to longer and more expensive design and permit processes, making it challenging to implement DC solutions efficiently.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: There is a shortage of skilled professionals familiar with DC technologies, which can hinder the deployment of DC solutions and increase project costs.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Regulatory frameworks for DC technologies are still evolving, making it challenging for stakeholders to navigate the complex regulatory landscape and implement DC solutions.

5. High costs of DC solutions: The initial investment for DC technologies can be higher compared to AC solutions, making it difficult to compete with established infrastructure and slowing the adoption of DC technologies.
