1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they directly impact the widespread adoption of DC technologies in various sectors, including ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, as outlined in the Shift2DC project.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization and interoperability between different DC components can lead to increased costs and complexity during installation and operation.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Misunderstandings about DC technologies can result in longer and more expensive design and permit processes, hindering the deployment of DC solutions.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The limited availability of skilled professionals familiar with DC technologies can lead to slower adoption and higher costs for DC projects.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: A lack of clear regulations and standards for DC systems can create uncertainty for investors and project developers, making it more difficult to plan and implement DC projects.

5. High costs of DC solutions: The higher initial costs of DC technologies compared to AC solutions can deter potential adopters and slow the transition to DC-based systems.
