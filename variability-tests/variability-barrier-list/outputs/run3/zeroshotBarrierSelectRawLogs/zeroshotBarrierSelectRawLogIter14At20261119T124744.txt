These five barriers have been selected as they directly impact the widespread adoption and implementation of DC technologies in various sectors. Incompatibility of components can hinder the seamless integration of DC systems, while misconceptions and lack of knowledge can lead to complex and costly design and permit processes. Lastly, high costs of DC solutions can make them less attractive compared to traditional AC systems, which may discourage their adoption.
