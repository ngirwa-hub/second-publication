1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

The selected barriers to the wider adoption of DC technologies in the Shift2DC project's focus sectors are:

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: As DC technologies become more common, ensuring compatibility between various DC components is crucial to prevent operational issues.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Misunderstanding and limited knowledge about DC technologies can lead to longer and more costly design and permit processes, potentially discouraging their widespread adoption.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: The success of DC technologies relies on skilled professionals who can design, install, and maintain the systems. However, there is a shortage of trained personnel, which could hinder the growth of the DC industry.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: A clear and consistent regulatory framework is essential for the development and deployment of DC technologies. However, the lack of a clear roadmap can create uncertainty and hinder investment in this area.

5. High costs of DC solutions: While the benefits of DC technologies are numerous, their initial costs can be prohibitive, making it challenging for businesses and individuals to adopt these solutions.

By addressing these five barriers, the Shift2DC project can help accelerate the transition to a more sustainable energy system through the promotion and implementation of DC technologies.
