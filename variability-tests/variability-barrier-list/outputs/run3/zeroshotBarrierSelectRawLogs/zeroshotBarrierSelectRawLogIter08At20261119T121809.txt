--- mistral_generalist ---
1. Incompatibility of DC systems components
2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process
3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers were selected as they represent significant challenges that need to be addressed to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies. Incompatibility of DC systems components is a key issue as it can lead to increased complexity and costs in system design and integration. Misconception and lack of knowledge can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, making the adoption of DC technologies more difficult and costly. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems is another challenge, as it can slow down the deployment and maintenance of DC systems. An uncertain regulatory roadmap can also create uncertainty for investors and project developers, making it harder to invest in and deploy DC technologies. High costs of DC solutions are a significant barrier to widespread adoption, as they can make these technologies less competitive with traditional AC technologies.
