3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap
5. High costs of DC solutions

These five barriers have been selected as they present significant challenges in promoting the use of direct current (DC) technologies in various sectors, as outlined in the Shift2DC project. The incompatibility of DC systems components can hinder the seamless integration of different components, making it difficult to achieve efficient and reliable operations. The misconception and lack of knowledge can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, potentially hindering the adoption of DC technologies. The lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems may result in insufficient expertise to design, install, and maintain DC systems, which can impact their performance and reliability. The uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and stakeholders, potentially deterring them from investing in DC technologies. Lastly, the high costs of DC solutions can be a significant barrier to their widespread adoption, as they may not be economically viable compared to conventional technologies.
