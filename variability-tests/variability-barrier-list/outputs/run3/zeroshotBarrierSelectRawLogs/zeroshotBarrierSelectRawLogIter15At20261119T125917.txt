These five barriers have been selected as they present significant challenges to the widespread adoption of DC technologies in the sectors targeted by the Shift2DC project.

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The lack of standardization and compatibility between different DC components can make it difficult and costly to integrate them into existing systems, slowing down the transition to DC technologies.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Limited understanding and misconceptions about DC technologies can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, potentially discouraging the adoption of DC solutions.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: There is a shortage of skilled professionals with expertise in DC systems, which can make it difficult to design, install, and maintain DC technologies, thereby hindering their widespread adoption.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Unclear regulations and policies regarding DC technologies can create uncertainty for investors and developers, making it more challenging to invest in and implement DC solutions.

5. High costs of DC solutions: The higher initial costs of DC solutions compared to their AC counterparts can be a significant barrier to their widespread adoption, particularly in areas with limited budgets for energy infrastructure upgrades.
