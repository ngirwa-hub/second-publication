--- mistral_generalist ---
Based on the provided context and my expertise, I would select the following five barriers:

1. Incompatibility of DC systems components: The diversity and lack of standardization of DC components can lead to compatibility issues and higher costs in the development and deployment of DC solutions.

2. Misconception and lack of knowledge leads to lengthy/expensive design and permit process: Limited understanding and awareness of DC technologies among designers, engineers, and regulators can result in longer and more costly design and permit processes.

3. Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems: Insufficient expertise in DC technologies among professionals in the energy sector can hinder the deployment and maintenance of DC systems.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Unclear or inconsistent regulations related to DC technologies can create uncertainty for investors and developers, hindering the adoption of DC solutions.

5. High costs of DC solutions: The initial costs of implementing DC technologies can be higher than those of AC solutions, making it difficult to compete with established technologies in the market.

These five barriers are particularly relevant to the Shift2DC project's focus on promoting the use of DC technologies in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. Addressing these barriers is crucial for accelerating the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.

4. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: Regulatory uncertainty can deter investments in DC technologies and create barriers to their widespread adoption.
5. High costs of DC solutions: The high cost of DC solutions compared to traditional AC technologies can be a significant barrier to their widespread adoption, particularly in the early stages of market development.

By addressing these five barriers, the Shift2DC project can help overcome some of the challenges faced in promoting the use of DC technologies and accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system.
