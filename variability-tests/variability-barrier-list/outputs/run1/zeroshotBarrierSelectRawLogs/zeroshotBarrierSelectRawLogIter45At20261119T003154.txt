These five barriers represent significant challenges in the transition to DC technologies, as identified by the Shift2DC project. The lack of clear use-cases demonstrating the advantages of DC technology, uncertainties in utility interactions, and the absence of pilot projects can hinder the adoption of DC solutions. In addition, the incompatibility of DC systems components can create technical challenges, while the high costs and the need for trained personnel can pose financial and human resource barriers. The regulatory environment also plays a role in the transition, as an uncertain regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for investors and stakeholders.
