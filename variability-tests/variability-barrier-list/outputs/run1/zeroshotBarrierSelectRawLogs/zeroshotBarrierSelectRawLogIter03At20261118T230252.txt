2. Uncertain utility interaction (net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards): Clarification and standardization of regulations and agreements between utilities and DC project developers are crucial for a smooth transition to DC technologies.

3. Lack of pilot projects: Real-world testing and demonstration of DC technologies in various sectors will help validate their potential and encourage broader adoption.

4. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: Overcoming public skepticism and fostering awareness and acceptance of DC technologies will be essential for their widespread adoption.

5. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Standardization and interoperability of DC components are necessary to ensure seamless integration and efficient operation of DC systems.

These barriers are significant obstacles in the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies and require attention and action to be addressed effectively.
