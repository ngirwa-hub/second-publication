--- mistral_generalist ---
Based on the provided context and my expertise, the five barriers I would select for the Shift2DC project are:

1. Lack of use-cases in which DC is advantageous: This barrier refers to the need for clear and compelling examples of situations where DC technologies offer significant benefits over AC (alternating current) solutions.

2. Uncertain utility interaction (net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards): This barrier highlights the importance of establishing clear guidelines and agreements between utilities and DC system users regarding net metering, utility ownership, and standards for DC systems.

3. Lack of pilot projects: Pilot projects are essential for demonstrating the viability and practicality of new technologies. In the case of DC technologies, a lack of pilot projects can hinder their adoption and slow down the transition to a sustainable energy system.

4. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: This barrier refers to the need to educate and engage the public about the benefits of DC technologies, and to encourage their support for the installation of DC systems in various sectors.

5. Incompatibility of DC systems components: This barrier highlights the need for standardization in DC systems components to ensure compatibility and interoperability between different components, which can facilitate the deployment and scaling of DC solutions.
