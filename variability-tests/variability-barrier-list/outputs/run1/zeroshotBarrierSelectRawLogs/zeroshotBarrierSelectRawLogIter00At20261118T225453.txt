--- mistral_generalist ---
Based on the given context, here are the five barriers I would select for the Shift2DC project:

1. Lack of use-cases in which DC is advantageous: Understanding where DC technologies provide a clear advantage over AC is crucial for driving their widespread adoption.

2. Uncertain utility interaction (net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards): Clarifying the rules and standards for integrating DC systems into the existing grid is essential for ensuring seamless operation and fair compensation for renewable energy producers.

3. Lack of pilot projects: Demonstrating the practical benefits and feasibility of DC technologies through pilot projects can help build confidence and encourage further investment.

4. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: Educating the public about the advantages of DC technologies and fostering support for their implementation is important for driving broader societal adoption.

5. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Ensuring that DC components are interoperable and can be easily integrated into existing systems is crucial for reducing costs and simplifying the adoption process.

By addressing these barriers, the Shift2DC project can help accelerate the transition to a more sustainable energy system through the promotion and implementation of DC technologies.
