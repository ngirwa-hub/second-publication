2. 3. Lack of pilot projects: This barrier refers to the absence of demonstrable proof-of-concept projects that showcase the practical benefits and feasibility of DC technologies in real-world applications.

4. Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects: This barrier relates to the general public's understanding and willingness to support DC projects, which can influence the adoption rate of DC technologies.

5. Incompatibility of DC systems components: This barrier refers to the challenge of ensuring compatibility among various components of DC systems, which can lead to integration issues and increased complexity in system design and implementation.
