5. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Ensuring that DC systems components are interoperable and compatible is essential for reducing costs, improving efficiency, and promoting the scalability of DC solutions.

These barriers were selected because they are significant challenges that the Shift2DC project aims to address in order to accelerate the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies.
