Based on the provided context and my role as an expert in DC technologies, I would select the following five barriers for the Shift2DC project:

1. Lack of use-cases in which DC is advantageous: This barrier refers to the absence of clear and convincing examples that demonstrate the benefits of using DC technologies over AC in specific applications.

2. Uncertain utility interaction (net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards): This barrier pertains to the unclear policies and standards regarding the interaction between DC systems and utilities, which can impact the adoption of DC technologies in various sectors.

3. Lack of pilot projects: This barrier highlights the need for more demonstration projects that showcase the practical applications and benefits of DC technologies, which can help build confidence and momentum in the industry.

4. Incompatibility of DC systems components: This barrier refers to the challenges in ensuring that different components of DC systems are compatible and can work together seamlessly, which can increase complexity and costs.

5. High costs of DC solutions: This barrier relates to the higher initial costs associated with DC technologies compared to AC, which can deter potential adopters due to financial considerations.
