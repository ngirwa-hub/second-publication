1. Lack of use-cases in which DC is advantageous

3. Lack of pilot projects

These five barriers are significant challenges in promoting the use of direct current (DC) technologies for a sustainable energy system. Addressing these obstacles will help accelerate the transition to DC-based solutions in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings as part of the Shift2DC project.

1. Lack of use-cases in which DC is advantageous: Identifying and demonstrating clear benefits of DC technologies over traditional AC systems will help promote their adoption in various sectors.

2. Uncertain utility interaction: Establishing clear guidelines for net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards will provide a predictable environment for DC solution providers and encourage investment in the technology.

3. Lack of pilot projects: Demonstrating successful DC projects in various sectors will help build confidence in the technology and provide practical examples of its benefits.

4. Public perception of DC: Improving public understanding and acceptance of DC technologies will increase the demand for installations and drive the development of new solutions.

5. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Ensuring compatibility between different DC components is essential for seamless integration and efficient operation of DC systems.

By addressing these barriers, the Shift2DC project can make significant progress in promoting the use of DC technologies for a sustainable energy system.
