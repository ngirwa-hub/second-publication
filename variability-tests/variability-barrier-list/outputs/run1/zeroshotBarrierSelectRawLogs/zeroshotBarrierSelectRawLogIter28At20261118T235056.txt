These five barriers have been selected as they present significant challenges in the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies, as outlined in the Shift2DC project. The first barrier, lack of use-cases, highlights the need for demonstrating the advantages of DC technologies over traditional AC systems. The second barrier, uncertain utility interaction, underscores the importance of clear guidelines for net metering, utility ownership, and standards to ensure seamless integration of DC systems. The third barrier, lack of pilot projects, is crucial for showcasing the practical applications and benefits of DC technologies. The fourth barrier, incompatibility of DC systems components, is a technical challenge that needs to be addressed for widespread adoption. Lastly, high costs of DC solutions present a significant financial barrier that must be addressed to make DC technologies more accessible and competitive.
