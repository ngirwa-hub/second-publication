These barriers have been selected as they directly impact the adoption and implementation of DC technologies in various sectors. Misconceptions and lack of knowledge can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, while the absence of clear use-cases where DC is advantageous can hinder its widespread adoption. Uncertain utility interaction, high costs, and the lack of pilot projects can create barriers for the integration of DC solutions in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. The lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems and incompatibility of DC systems components can also pose challenges in implementing and maintaining DC systems effectively.
