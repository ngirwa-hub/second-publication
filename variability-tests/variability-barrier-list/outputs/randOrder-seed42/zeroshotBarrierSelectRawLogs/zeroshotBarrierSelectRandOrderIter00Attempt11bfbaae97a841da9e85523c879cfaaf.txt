- uncertain regulatory roadmap
- incompatibility of DC systems components
- lack of pilot projects

These barriers pose significant challenges in the widespread adoption of direct current (DC) technologies. High costs can make DC solutions less competitive compared to traditional AC systems, while misconceptions and lack of knowledge can lead to costly and time-consuming design and permit processes. Uncertain regulatory roadmaps can create uncertainty for investors and project developers, and incompatibility of DC systems components can hinder the development of interoperable systems. A lack of pilot projects can limit the availability of real-world evidence demonstrating the benefits of DC technologies, making it more difficult for the industry to gain traction.
