These barriers pose significant challenges in the widespread adoption of direct current (DC) technologies for renewable energy applications. Uncertain regulatory roadmaps create unpredictability for investors and project developers, while power losses and quality and safety issues can affect the efficiency and reliability of DC systems. Incompatibility of DC systems components can hinder the integration and interoperability of various devices, and uncertain utility interaction can complicate net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards. Additionally, the high costs of DC solutions can deter potential adopters, especially in comparison to established, less efficient alternatives.
