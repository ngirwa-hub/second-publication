--- mistral-subject-matter ---
- incompatibility of DC systems components
- uncertain regulatory roadmap
- lack of pilot projects
- uncertain utility interaction (net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards)
- high costs of DC solutions

These barriers have been selected as they are key challenges in the transition to DC technologies. The incompatibility of DC systems components can hinder the seamless integration of DC components, uncertain regulatory roadmaps can create uncertainty for investors and project developers, lack of pilot projects can hinder the demonstration of the benefits and feasibility of DC technologies, uncertain utility interaction can create challenges in integrating DC systems with existing grid infrastructure, and high costs of DC solutions can make them less competitive compared to traditional AC solutions.
