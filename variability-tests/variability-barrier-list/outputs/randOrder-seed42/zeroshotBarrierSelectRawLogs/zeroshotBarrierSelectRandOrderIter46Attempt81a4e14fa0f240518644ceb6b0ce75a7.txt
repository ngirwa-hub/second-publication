These barriers have been selected as they present significant challenges to the widespread adoption of direct current (DC) technologies in the Shift2DC project sectors. Incompatibility of components, reduced reliability, and power losses are technical issues that need to be addressed to ensure the efficiency and effectiveness of DC systems. Lack of trained personnel and an uncertain regulatory roadmap are barriers that hinder the development and implementation of DC solutions.
