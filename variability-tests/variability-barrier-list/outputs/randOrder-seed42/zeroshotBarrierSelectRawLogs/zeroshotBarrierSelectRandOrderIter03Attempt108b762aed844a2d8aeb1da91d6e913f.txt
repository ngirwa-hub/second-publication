--- mistral-subject-matter ---
- high costs of DC solutions
- power losses, quality and safety issues
- uncertain utility interaction (net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards)
- uncertain regulatory roadmap
- lack of pilot projects

These barriers have been selected as they present significant challenges in the adoption and implementation of DC technologies. High costs and uncertain regulatory roadmaps can deter potential investors and stakeholders. Power losses, quality, and safety issues can impact the efficiency and reliability of DC systems. Lack of pilot projects can hinder the demonstration of DC technology's practicality and effectiveness. Uncertain utility interaction can create complications in the integration of DC systems into existing power grids.
