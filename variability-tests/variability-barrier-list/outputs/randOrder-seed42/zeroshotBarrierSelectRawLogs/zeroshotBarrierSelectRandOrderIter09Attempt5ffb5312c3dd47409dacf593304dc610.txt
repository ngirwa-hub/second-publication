--- mistral-generalist ---
- Lack of enough trained personnel in DC systems
- Uncertain utility interaction (net metering, utility ownership, and agreed standards)
- Public perception of DC and readiness to 'champion' installations from DC projects
- Lack of pilot projects
- High costs of DC solutions

These barriers present challenges in the transition to a sustainable energy system using DC technologies, as outlined by the Shift2DC project. The first four barriers hinder the adoption and implementation of DC solutions, while the last barrier relates to the financial aspect, which can impact the widespread use of these technologies. Addressing these barriers is crucial for accelerating the transition to a sustainable energy system with DC technologies.
