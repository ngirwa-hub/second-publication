The selected barriers are critical to address for the successful implementation of the Shift2DC project. Ensuring clear utility interaction, addressing high costs, clarifying regulatory roadmaps, addressing component incompatibility, and improving the availability of trained personnel will contribute to overcoming challenges in the adoption of DC technologies. Additionally, addressing power losses, quality, and safety issues will further enhance the reliability and efficiency of DC systems.
