These five barriers have been selected as they directly impact the adoption and implementation of direct current (DC) technologies in various sectors. Reduced reliability in DC devices, incompatibility of components, and high costs are technical challenges that need to be addressed. Misconception and lack of knowledge can lead to lengthy and expensive design and permit processes, while uncertain utility interaction and regulatory roadmap can create uncertainty for stakeholders involved in DC projects.
