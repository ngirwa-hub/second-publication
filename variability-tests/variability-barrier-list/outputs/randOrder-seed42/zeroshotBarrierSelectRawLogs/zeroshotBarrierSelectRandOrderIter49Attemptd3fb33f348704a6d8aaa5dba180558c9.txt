These barriers present challenges in the widespread adoption of direct current (DC) technologies, as outlined in the Shift2DC project.

1. Public perception: The acceptance and support of the public are crucial for the successful implementation of DC projects. However, there may be a lack of understanding and readiness to champion DC installations, which can hinder their deployment.

2. Power losses, quality, and safety issues: DC power systems can offer efficiency advantages compared to AC systems, but they still face challenges related to power losses, ensuring quality, and maintaining safety.

3. Uncertain regulatory roadmap: A clear and consistent regulatory framework is necessary for the efficient and widespread adoption of DC technologies. However, there is often uncertainty regarding the regulatory environment, which can deter potential adopters.

4. High costs of DC solutions: Despite the potential benefits of DC technologies, their implementation can be cost-prohibitive, which can make it difficult for projects to secure funding and move forward.

5. Incompatibility of DC systems components: Ensuring compatibility between various DC systems components is essential for seamless integration and operation. However, the lack of interoperability can present challenges and increase costs during the design and implementation phases.
