- lack of pilot projects

These barriers have been selected as they are critical challenges that need to be addressed for the successful implementation and widespread adoption of DC technologies in various sectors. The uncertainty surrounding utility interactions, regulatory frameworks, and component compatibility can create hurdles in the development and deployment of DC solutions. High costs and the lack of pilot projects can hinder the financial viability and proof of concept for these technologies. Lastly, the lack of trained personnel and knowledge about DC systems can lead to inefficient design processes and delays in project implementation.
