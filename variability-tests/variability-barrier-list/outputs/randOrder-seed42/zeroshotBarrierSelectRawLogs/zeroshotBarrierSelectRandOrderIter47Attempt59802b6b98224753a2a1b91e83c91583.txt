- Uncertain regulatory roadmap

These barriers hinder the accelerated transition to a sustainable energy system through the promotion of DC technologies. Lack of pilot projects, high costs, incompatibility of components, uncertain utility interaction, and unclear regulatory roadmap present significant challenges in the adoption and implementation of DC solutions in the sectors of ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. Addressing these barriers is crucial for the success of the Shift2DC project and the broader goal of promoting DC technologies for a sustainable energy system.
